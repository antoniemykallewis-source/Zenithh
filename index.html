Explore all FAQs
Is a captain and crew included?

Yes. Zenith’s private charters are crewed. You do not need a boating licence. Your captain and crew take care of navigation and safety.

How far ahead should I book?

For Singapore, Zenith recommends booking two to four weeks ahead, especially during peak periods. Ask the team about your preferred destination and date.

Can we bring food and drinks?

Singapore guests can bring refreshments or arrange catering with Zenith. Inclusions and any corkage fees vary by yacht and destination, so check the yacht’s package details.

What happens if the weather is bad?

In Singapore, guests should still arrive at the meeting point for instructions. The captain decides whether conditions are safe. Unsafe conditions may lead to a mutually agreed postponement.

What is included in the charter price?

The yacht and professional crew are included. Food, drinks, water toys, decor and other inclusions vary by vessel and package. The team confirms the full quote before booking.
