Frequently Asked Questions

About Zenith

Who is Zenith Yacht Charters?

Zenith Yacht Charters is a Singapore-based luxury yacht charter company offering private, crewed charters and boat rentals across Southeast Asia. Beyond charters, we also provide yacht ownership, sales and brokerage, and yacht management services. Our focus is simple: unforgettable experiences on the water, handled end to end.

Where does Zenith operate?

We currently offer private yacht charters in Singapore, Malaysia, and Thailand, with each destination offering its own coastlines, islands, and experiences. You can explore each location through our regional pages, or speak to our team to plan a charter in any of them.

What makes Zenith different from other yacht charter companies?

We combine a well-maintained luxury fleet with experienced crew and genuinely personal service, tailoring every charter to the occasion rather than offering one-size-fits-all packages. Our pricing is transparent with no hidden fees, and we handle every detail from enquiry to disembarkation.

Is Zenith a licensed and insured charter operator?

Yes. Zenith Yacht Charters Pte Ltd is a licensed operator (TA03727), and our yachts are operated by professional, experienced crew with safety as the priority. You’re in trusted hands from the moment you board.

Choosing a destination

Which destination should I choose — Singapore, Malaysia, or Thailand?

It depends on the experience you’re after. Singapore is ideal for city-skyline cruises, celebrations, and corporate events close to the marina; Thailand offers dramatic island scenery around Phuket and Phang Nga Bay; and Malaysia brings relaxed island cruising. Our team is happy to recommend the best fit for your group and occasion.

How do your charter experiences differ across Singapore, Malaysia, and Thailand?

Each destination has its own departure points, cruising routes, and highlights, so the itineraries and scenery differ considerably. What stays consistent is the Zenith standard: a luxury yacht, professional crew, and a tailored experience. Visit the relevant country page for local details.

Can I book charters in more than one country through Zenith?

Yes. As a regional operator, we can help you arrange charters across Singapore, Malaysia, and Thailand through a single point of contact. This is especially useful for travellers combining destinations or planning multi-leg trips.

When is the best time of year to charter across Southeast Asia?

Southeast Asia is a year-round yachting region, though the ideal window varies by country due to differing monsoon seasons. Generally, conditions are calmest outside the peak monsoon months in each location. For destination-specific timing, see the individual country pages or ask our team.

How chartering works

What is a private yacht charter, and how is it different from a shared boat tour?

A private charter means the yacht is exclusively yours for the booking — just your group, your pace, and your preferences, with no strangers on board. Unlike a fixed shared tour, the experience is tailored around your occasion, whether that’s a celebration, a corporate event, or a quiet day at sea.

Are your charters crewed, or do I sail the yacht myself?

All Zenith charters come with a professional captain and crew, so you don’t need any boating licence or experience. You simply relax and enjoy while our team handles navigation, safety, and service.

What types of occasions do you cater to?

We host a wide range of occasions, including birthdays , weddings and proposals, corporate events and team building, family gatherings, and relaxed leisure cruises. Whatever the event, we tailor the yacht, route, and add-ons to suit it.

What is generally included in a Zenith charter?

Every charter includes the yacht and a professional crew, with transparent pricing and no hidden fees. Food and beverage, water toys, decorations, and other extras can be added to customise your experience. Exact inclusions vary by yacht and package, which our team will confirm on enquiry.

How do I get a quote or make an enquiry?

Simply reach out through our contact form, WhatsApp, phone, or email, and our team will respond promptly with options and pricing. We’ll guide you through choosing the right yacht, destination, and package for your plans.

Fleet

What kinds of yachts are in the Zenith fleet across the region?

Our fleet spans luxury monohulls, sailing and power catamarans, and motor yachts, ranging from intimate vessels for small groups to large yachts for sizeable events. Options and capacities vary by destination, so browse the country pages to see what’s available where you’d like to sail.

Can Zenith accommodate large groups or corporate events?

Absolutely. Several yachts in our fleet are built for larger gatherings, comfortably hosting sizeable groups for parties, corporate functions, and team-building days. Our team will match you with the right vessel and help coordinate catering and add-ons.

Other services

Does Zenith offer yacht ownership opportunities?

Does Zenith provide yacht sales and brokerage?

Yes. Our dedicated yacht sales and brokerage team can assist whether you’re buying or selling a yacht. Reach out to our brokerage contact for a confidential discussion.

Does Zenith offer yacht management services?

Yes. We provide professional yacht management to keep your vessel maintained, compliant, and ready to sail. Our management team can tailor a service package to your needs.

Getting started

How do I contact the right team?

Zenith has dedicated contacts for charter enquiries, yacht sales and brokerage, yacht management, and marketing or collaborations. You’ll find the relevant phone number and email for each on our contact page, so your query reaches the right people quickly.

Yes, we welcome partnerships with travel agents, corporate clients, and event organisers. Contact our team to discuss collaboration and tailored arrangements.
