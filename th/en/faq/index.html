Thailand Yacht Charter FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Planning a Thailand Charter

How much does it cost to rent a yacht in Phuket?

Our Thailand charters are priced in Thai baht and vary by yacht, duration, and season, with packages starting from around ฿20,000 for our sailing catamaran. Speedboats and larger motor yachts are priced accordingly for their size and capacity. Browse our Phuket fleet for starting rates, or contact us for a tailored quote.

Where do your yacht charters depart from in Phuket?

Most charters depart from Chalong Pier, one of Phuket’s main launch points for island trips, though your exact departure point can vary by yacht. We’ll confirm the pier and boarding time when you book so you know exactly where to meet.

Do you offer hotel or resort transfers from Phuket?

Yes, we can help arrange transfers from your hotel in areas like Patong, Kata, or Karon to the departure pier. Just let our team know your accommodation when booking and we’ll coordinate the pick-up.

How far in advance should I book during Thailand's high season?

The Andaman high season (roughly November to April) is busy, so we recommend booking two to four weeks ahead to secure your preferred yacht and date. Earlier is better for peak periods and public holidays. You can enquire here to check availability.

Can you arrange a last-minute yacht charter in Phuket?

Often yes, depending on fleet availability on your dates. Reach out via WhatsApp or our contact page and we’ll do our best to get you on the water quickly.

Islands & Itineraries

Which islands can we visit on a yacht charter from Phuket?

From Phuket you can explore a huge range of islands, including the famous Phi Phi Islands, Coral Island, Racha Yai, Khai Island, Maithon Island, and Naka Island. Explore all the destinations on our Discover pages to plan your ideal route.

Can we cruise to Phang Nga Bay and James Bond Island in a day?

Yes. A day charter can take you into the dramatic limestone scenery of Phang Nga Bay and the iconic James Bond Island. Travel time depends on your departure point and vessel, which our team will factor into your itinerary.

Is a Phi Phi Islands day trip possible by private yacht?

Absolutely — the Phi Phi Islands are one of our most requested private-charter routes, ideal for swimming, snorkeling, and photos in the turquoise bays. A faster vessel like a speedboat gets
you there with more time to explore.

What's the best island route for a first-time visitor to Phuket?

First-timers often love a mix of easy-to-reach spots like Coral Island and Racha Yai for clear water and snorkeling, or the scenery of Phang Nga Bay. Our crew will recommend a route based on your group, timing, and the day’s conditions.

Can we do a sunset cruise over the Andaman Sea?

Yes, a sunset cruise is a beautiful way to enjoy the Andaman Sea, with calm evening waters and golden views. It’s popular for couples, celebrations, and relaxed evenings on the water — just ask us to arrange one.

Do you offer overnight or multi-day island-hopping charters?

Yes, several of our yachts are suited to longer island-hopping trips beyond a single day. Contact our team with your dates and the islands you’d like to see, and we’ll design a multi-day itinerary.

Private Charter vs Join Trip

What's the difference between a private charter and a Join Trip day trip?

A private charter gives you exclusive use of the yacht for your group, with a route tailored to you. A Join Trip is an affordable, ticketed day trip you share with other guests on a set itinerary — great value for solo travellers, couples, and small groups.

Are your Join Trip day trips all-inclusive?

Yes, our Join Trip day trips are designed to be hassle-free and all-inclusive, so you can simply turn up and enjoy the day. See what’s covered on the Join Trip page.

Which is better for my group — a private yacht or a shared trip?

If you want privacy, flexibility, and a custom route, choose a private charter; if you’re after the best value and don’t mind a fixed itinerary with other guests, a Join Trip is ideal. Tell us your group size and budget and we’ll point you to the right option.

Boats & Capacity

Should I choose a speedboat, sailing catamaran, or motor yacht in Phuket?

A speedboat is fast and fun for covering more islands in a day, a sailing catamaran offers spacious, relaxed cruising for larger groups, and a motor yacht delivers premium comfort and space. You can compare all three on our fleet page and we’ll help you match the vessel to your plans.

How many guests can your Thailand yachts carry?

Capacity ranges from around 8 guests on our speedboat to 35 on our sailing catamaran, so we can cater to intimate outings and larger celebrations alike. Exact capacity is listed on each yacht on our fleet page.

Which yacht is best for island hopping vs a relaxed cruise?

For fast-paced island hopping, a speedboat covers more ground; for a leisurely day of sunbathing, swimming, and dining, a catamaran or motor yacht is more comfortable. Our team will recommend the best fit once we know your itinerary.

On the Water — Activities

Is snorkeling gear included on the charter?

Snorkeling gear is provided on many of our yachts so you can explore the reefs and clear waters around the islands. We’ll confirm the equipment onboard your chosen vessel when you book.

Can we go scuba diving during the trip?

Yes, we can arrange scuba excursions as part of your experience for those who want to explore beneath the surface. Let us know when booking and we’ll coordinate the dive and equipment.

Do you offer deep-sea fishing charters in Phuket?

We do — deep-sea fishing trips are one of our Thailand experiences, perfect for a catch-and-enjoy day out on the Andaman Sea. Ask our team to add fishing to your charter.

What is Yona Beach Club and how do we visit it?

Yona Beach Club is Phuket’s renowned floating beach club, featuring an infinity pool, cabanas, live DJs, cocktails, and dining set against the Andaman Sea. You can visit it as part of a trip with us, with packages starting from ฿2,000.

Are water toys and paddleboards provided?

Many of our yachts come equipped with extras like paddleboards, swim floats, and beach chairs to make the most of your day. Availability varies by vessel, so we’ll confirm what’s included on yours.

Costs, Fees & What’s Included

What's included in a Thailand yacht charter price?

Every private charter includes the yacht and a professional captain and crew, with transparent pricing and no hidden fees. Extras such as food and beverage, water sports, and activities can be added to tailor your day. Exact inclusions vary by yacht and package, which we’ll confirm on enquiry.

Are national park entrance fees included?

National park entrance fees for areas like the Phi Phi Islands and Phang Nga Bay are usually charged separately from the charter price, as set by the Thai authorities. We’ll let you know which fees apply to your chosen route so there are no surprises on the day.

Is food and drink included, or can we arrange catering onboard?

You can add a food and beverage package to your charter, or we can tailor catering to your preferences. Let us know any dietary requirements and we’ll arrange the menu ahead of your trip.

What payment methods and currency do you accept?

Our Thailand charters are quoted in Thai baht, and our team will guide you through the accepted payment options when you book. Reach out via our contact page for details.

Practical & Seasonal

When is the best time of year to charter a yacht in Phuket?

The Andaman Sea’s high season runs roughly from November to April, offering calm seas, clear skies, and the best conditions for island hopping. Charters still run in the May–October green season, though weather can be more variable. We’ll advise the best timing for your plans.

Is it safe to sail during Thailand's monsoon season?

Safety is always our priority, and our experienced crew monitor conditions closely, adjusting routes to sheltered areas when needed. If weather makes sailing unsafe, we’ll work with you on alternatives.

What happens if my charter is cancelled due to weather?

In the event of severe weather, we offer flexible rescheduling so you don’t lose out. Our team will contact you and help arrange an alternative date.

Do I need a boating licence to charter in Thailand?

No — all our charters come with a professional captain and crew, so no licence or experience is required. You simply relax and enjoy the day while we handle the navigation.

Are your charters suitable for families with children?

Yes, our charters are family-friendly, and our crew take care to make the day safe and enjoyable for all ages. Let us know if you’re bringing young children so we can ensure suitable safety equipment is on board.

What should I bring for a day on the water in Phuket?

Pack sunscreen, a hat, sunglasses, swimwear, a towel, and a light cover-up for sun protection, plus a camera for the scenery. We’ll provide the rest, and can advise on anything specific to your itinerary.
