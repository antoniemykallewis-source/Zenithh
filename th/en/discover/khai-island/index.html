Khai Islands Yacht Charter from Phuket

Discover the Highlights of Khai Islands

The Khai Islands are a group of small tropical islands located east of Phuket, comprising Khai Nok, Khai Nai, and Khai Nui. Known for their sandy beaches, clear turquoise waters, and marine life, the islands offer an enjoyable setting for swimming, snorkeling, and relaxing on the Andaman Sea.

Their close proximity to Phuket makes a Khai Islands yacht charter a convenient choice for a day at sea, allowing you to visit multiple islands while enjoying a flexible Phuket island-hopping yacht trip.

Khai Islands
Khai Nok Island

Khai Nok Island is the largest and one of the most visited of the three Khai Islands, featuring a sandy shoreline surrounded by shallow turquoise waters. Visitors can spend time swimming, relaxing by the beach, or enjoying the tropical scenery.

Khai Nok can be included as part of a private yacht charter to the Khai Islands, with the itinerary continuing to other nearby islands and snorkeling areas.

Khai Islands
Khai Nai Island

Khai Nai Island offers sandy shores and clear, shallow waters where visitors can swim and snorkel. The surrounding waters provide opportunities to observe tropical fish and enjoy the coastal scenery.

Combining Khai Nai with Khai Nok and Khai Nui creates a varied Khai Islands yacht trip within easy cruising distance of Phuket.

Khai Islands
Khai Nui Island

Unlike the neighboring islands, Khai Nui Island is a small rocky island without a main sandy beach. Its surrounding waters are known for marine life and coral formations, making snorkeling the primary attraction.

A Khai Islands yacht charter from Phuket allows guests to incorporate Khai Nui as a snorkeling stop while exploring the three islands as part of the same journey.

Khai Islands
Crystal-Clear Waters

The waters surrounding the Khai Islands offer opportunities for swimming and snorkeling, with tropical fish and underwater scenery found around different areas of the islands.

A Khai Islands snorkeling trip by yacht provides the flexibility to visit different locations according to your itinerary and local sea conditions, while also allowing plenty of time to relax on board between stops.

Khai Islands
Tropical Island Escape

With three islands located close to one another, the Khai Islands are particularly well suited to Phuket island hopping by yacht. Guests can combine beaches, swimming, snorkeling, and scenic cruising within a single private charter itinerary.

Their location near Phuket also makes the islands suitable for families, couples, groups of friends, and guests looking for a convenient private yacht charter from Phuket.

Explore the Khai Islands by Yacht

From the beaches of Khai Nok and Khai Nai to the snorkeling waters around Khai Nui, the Khai Islands offer several different experiences within a relatively compact island group.

Exploring on a Khai Islands yacht charter allows you to visit multiple islands, discover snorkeling spots, swim in clear waters, and relax on board while cruising through the Andaman Sea.
