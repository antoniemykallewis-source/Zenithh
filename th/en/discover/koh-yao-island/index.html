Koh Yao Islands Yacht Charter

Discover the Highlights of Koh Yao Islands

The Koh Yao Islands, comprising Koh Yao Yai and Koh Yao Noi, are located between Phuket and Krabi within the scenic waters of Phang Nga Bay. Known for their quiet beaches, green landscapes, fishing communities, and relaxed atmosphere, the islands offer a different experience from Phuket’s busier island destinations.

A Koh Yao Islands yacht charter provides a comfortable and flexible way to explore the coastline, enjoy peaceful anchorages, and experience the distinctive scenery of Phang Nga Bay as part of a private journey from Phuket.

Koh Yao Islands
Koh Yao Yai

Koh Yao Yai is the larger of the two main islands, featuring stretches of coastline, tropical vegetation, local communities, and scenic views across the surrounding waters. Its relaxed atmosphere makes it well suited to visitors looking for a quieter island experience.

A private yacht charter to Koh Yao Yai allows you to combine coastal cruising, beach stops, and time relaxing on board while exploring the waters between Phuket and Phang Nga Bay.

Koh Yao Islands
Koh Yao Noi

Koh Yao Noi is known for its fishing communities, rural landscapes, local restaurants, and views of the limestone formations scattered throughout Phang Nga Bay. The island offers an opportunity to experience a slower-paced side of southern Thailand.

Including Koh Yao Noi in a Koh Yao Islands yacht trip allows you to combine local island experiences with scenic cruising and other destinations around the bay.

Koh Yao Islands
Secluded Beaches

The Koh Yao Islands feature numerous stretches of peaceful coastline where visitors can enjoy swimming, paddleboarding, or simply relaxing by the water, depending on local conditions.

Exploring on a Koh Yao Islands yacht charter from Phuket provides greater flexibility to discover different parts of the coastline while enjoying the privacy and comfort of your yacht.

Koh Yao Islands
Scenic Phang Nga Bay Views

Located within Phang Nga Bay, the Koh Yao Islands are surrounded by distinctive limestone formations, tropical islands, and emerald waters. Cruising around the islands provides panoramic views of one of southern Thailand’s most recognizable coastal landscapes.

The islands can also form part of a wider Phang Nga Bay yacht charter, connecting your journey with other destinations throughout the bay.

Koh Yao Islands
Authentic Island Experience

Koh Yao Yai and Koh Yao Noi retain a strong sense of local island life, with fishing communities, rural landscapes, local food, and a more relaxed pace than many heavily visited resort areas.

A private yacht charter from Phuket to Koh Yao Islands allows guests to combine the scenery of Phang Nga Bay with opportunities to experience the islands and their local surroundings.

Explore the Koh Yao Islands by Yacht

From the peaceful coastlines of Koh Yao Yai to the local character of Koh Yao Noi and the limestone scenery of Phang Nga Bay, the Koh Yao Islands offer a distinctive destination within easy cruising reach of Phuket.

Exploring on a Koh Yao Islands yacht charter gives you the flexibility to combine island visits, coastal cruising, swimming, sightseeing, and time relaxing on board as part of your preferred itinerary.
