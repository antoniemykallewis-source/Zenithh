Koh Hong Krabi Yacht Charter

Discover the Highlights of Koh Hong Krabi

Koh Hong Krabi is a striking island destination known for its emerald lagoon, towering limestone cliffs, sandy beaches, and clear tropical waters. Located within Than Bok Khorani National Park, the island offers opportunities for swimming, snorkeling, kayaking, and sightseeing amid the distinctive limestone scenery of the Andaman Sea.

A Koh Hong Krabi yacht charter offers a comfortable way to experience the island, combining scenic cruising with time at Hong Lagoon, the beach, and surrounding waters as part of a private itinerary.

Koh Hong Krabi
Hong Lagoon

Hong Lagoon is one of Koh Hong’s most distinctive natural attractions, enclosed by towering limestone cliffs and connected to the sea through a narrow entrance. Water levels and accessibility vary with the tides, creating a unique setting surrounded by dramatic rock formations.

Visiting the lagoon as part of a private yacht charter to Koh Hong allows you to combine this natural attraction with other highlights around the island.

Koh Hong Krabi
Hong Beach

Hong Beach features a sandy shoreline surrounded by turquoise waters, limestone formations, and tropical vegetation. Its scenic setting makes it an inviting place for swimming, relaxing by the beach, and enjoying the natural surroundings.

Hong Beach can be incorporated into a Koh Hong Krabi yacht trip, combining time ashore with cruising and other activities around the island.

Koh Hong Krabi
Kayaking Through Limestone Cliffs

Koh Hong’s limestone formations, sheltered waterways, and lagoon make kayaking a popular way to experience the island from the water. Depending on tides and local conditions, visitors can paddle through scenic areas surrounded by towering cliffs.

A Koh Hong yacht charter from Krabi can combine kayaking with beach visits, swimming, and sightseeing as part of a flexible island itinerary.

Koh Hong Krabi
Snorkeling & Marine Life

The waters around Koh Hong provide opportunities for snorkeling and discovering tropical marine life in the Andaman Sea. Conditions vary between locations and throughout the year, so snorkeling stops can be selected according to the conditions during your trip.

Combining snorkeling with a Krabi yacht charter to Koh Hong allows guests to experience both the island’s coastal scenery and its underwater environment.

Koh Hong Krabi
Panoramic Viewpoint

The Koh Hong viewpoint offers elevated views across the surrounding islands, turquoise waters, and dramatic limestone landscapes. Reaching the viewpoint involves a climb, but visitors are rewarded with an impressive perspective over the Hong Islands and the surrounding Andaman Sea.

The viewpoint can add a land-based experience to a Koh Hong Krabi yacht charter, complementing time spent cruising, swimming, and relaxing on the water.

Explore Koh Hong Krabi by Yacht

From Hong Lagoon and its limestone cliffs to sandy beaches, snorkeling areas, and panoramic island views, Koh Hong offers a varied experience for visitors exploring the Krabi coastline.

A Koh Hong Krabi yacht charter gives you the flexibility to combine sightseeing, kayaking, swimming, snorkeling, and time relaxing on board as part of your preferred itinerary.
