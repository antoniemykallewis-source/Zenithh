Coral Island Yacht Charter from Phuket

Discover the Highlights of Coral Island

Coral Island, also known as Koh Hae, is a popular tropical escape just south of Phuket, known for its sandy beaches, clear waters, coral reefs, and variety of water activities. Its convenient location makes it an excellent choice for swimming, snorkeling, relaxing by the beach, and Phuket island hopping by yacht. A Coral Island yacht charter offers a comfortable way to experience the island while enjoying the freedom to explore at your own pace.

Coral Island
Long Beach

Long Beach is one of Coral Island’s best-known stretches of coastline, offering a spacious sandy beach and inviting turquoise waters. Visitors can enjoy swimming, sunbathing, or simply relaxing while taking in views across the Andaman Sea. The beach is a convenient stop during a private yacht charter to Coral Island from Phuket.

Coral Island
Banana Beach

Banana Beach offers a picturesque tropical setting surrounded by clear waters and lush greenery. The area is popular for swimming, snorkeling, paddleboarding, and relaxing by the shore. Its location makes it a great addition to a Coral Island yacht trip or a wider Phuket island-hopping itinerary.

Coral Island
Snorkeling & Coral Reefs

Coral Island is surrounded by coral reefs and tropical marine life, making snorkeling one of the island’s most popular activities. A Coral Island yacht charter from Phuket allows you to combine snorkeling with swimming, beach visits, and time relaxing on board as part of a flexible day itinerary.

Coral Island
Water Activities

Visitors to Coral Island can enjoy a range of water activities, including kayaking, paddleboarding, parasailing, and other recreational experiences offered by local operators. These activities can easily complement a Phuket yacht charter itinerary, whether you prefer an adventurous day on the water or a more leisurely island escape.

Coral Island
Tropical Beach Escape

With its sandy beaches, tropical scenery, and easy access from Phuket, Coral Island is an attractive destination for a relaxing day at sea. Its convenient location also makes it a natural stop during a Phuket island-hopping yacht trip for families, couples, groups of friends, and private celebrations.

Explore Coral Island by Yacht

Discover Coral Island’s beaches, snorkeling spots, coral reefs, and tropical scenery aboard a Coral Island yacht charter. Exploring by yacht gives you greater flexibility to enjoy the island while combining swimming, snorkeling, water activities, and relaxing on board.
