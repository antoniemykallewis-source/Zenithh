Racha Yai Island Yacht Charter from Phuket

Discover the Highlights of Racha Yai Island

Racha Yai Island is a popular island destination south of Phuket, known for its turquoise waters, sandy beaches, coral reefs, and excellent opportunities for snorkeling and diving. Located around 20 kilometers from Phuket, the island combines beautiful coastal scenery with a variety of activities both above and below the water.

A Racha Yai Island yacht charter offers a comfortable and flexible way to explore its beaches, bays, and snorkeling spots while enjoying a scenic journey across the Andaman Sea from Phuket.

Racha Yai Island
Patok Bay

Patok Bay is one of Racha Yai Island’s best-known beaches, featuring a sandy shoreline surrounded by clear turquoise waters. Its picturesque setting makes it a popular place for swimming, relaxing by the beach, and enjoying views across the Andaman Sea.

Patok Bay can be included as part of a private yacht charter to Racha Yai Island, combining time ashore with swimming and relaxing on board.

Racha Yai Island
Siam Bay

Siam Bay is known for its clear waters and underwater scenery, making it a popular location for snorkeling around Racha Yai Island. Visitors may encounter tropical fish and coral formations while exploring the sheltered waters of the bay.

A Racha Yai Island yacht trip can combine Siam Bay with other beaches and snorkeling locations around the island as part of a flexible day itinerary.

Racha Yai Island
World-Class Snorkeling & Diving

Racha Yai Island is one of the established snorkeling and diving destinations near Phuket, with several dive and snorkeling sites located around its coastline. Clear-water conditions can provide good underwater visibility and opportunities to observe tropical marine life and coral formations.

A Racha Yai Island yacht charter from Phuket allows guests to combine time in the water with island exploration, coastal cruising, and relaxing on board.

Racha Yai Island
Ter Bay

Located on the eastern side of Racha Yai Island, Ter Bay offers a quieter coastal setting surrounded by tropical scenery. Depending on weather and sea conditions, the bay can provide opportunities for swimming, paddleboarding, snorkeling, and relaxing on the water.

Its location makes Ter Bay a scenic addition to a Phuket yacht charter itinerary exploring Racha Yai Island.

Racha Yai Island
Scenic Yacht Anchorage

Racha Yai Island features several bays and anchorages surrounded by clear waters and attractive coastal scenery. Depending on conditions and the selected itinerary, guests can spend time swimming, enjoying water activities, having lunch on board, or simply relaxing at sea.

These surroundings make Racha Yai a popular destination for a private yacht charter from Phuket, whether for a dedicated island trip or as part of a wider island-hopping journey.

Explore Racha Yai Island by Yacht
