Bamboo Island Yacht Charter near Phi Phi

Discover the Highlights of Bamboo Island

Bamboo Island, also known as Koh Mai Phai, is a tropical island located near the Phi Phi Islands in the Andaman Sea. Known for its sandy shoreline, turquoise waters, and surrounding coral reefs, the island offers a scenic setting for swimming, snorkeling, and relaxing by the sea.

A Bamboo Island yacht charter provides a comfortable way to experience the island while combining beach time, snorkeling, and scenic cruising. Its location near Phi Phi also makes Bamboo Island a natural addition to a Phi Phi island-hopping yacht trip.

Bamboo Island
Pristine White-Sand Beach

Bamboo Island features a broad stretch of soft white sand surrounded by clear tropical waters. The beach provides an inviting setting for swimming, sunbathing, walking along the shoreline, or simply enjoying the surrounding Andaman Sea scenery.

Visiting as part of a private yacht charter to Bamboo Island allows you to combine time ashore with other destinations around the Phi Phi Islands.

Bamboo Island
Crystal-Clear Waters

The clear waters surrounding Bamboo Island provide excellent opportunities for swimming and enjoying the tropical surroundings. Depending on weather and sea conditions, guests can spend time in the water or simply relax on board while taking in views of the island.

A Bamboo Island yacht trip provides the flexibility to combine swimming with beach visits, snorkeling, and cruising around the surrounding islands.

Bamboo Island
Coral Reefs & Snorkeling

The waters around Bamboo Island feature coral reefs and a variety of tropical marine life, making snorkeling a popular activity during a visit.

A Bamboo Island snorkeling trip by yacht allows guests to explore selected snorkeling areas while combining the experience with swimming, beach time, and other stops around the Phi Phi Islands.

Bamboo Island
Peaceful Island Escape

With its tropical coastline and natural surroundings, Bamboo Island provides a relaxing contrast to some of the busier attractions around the Phi Phi Islands.

Including Bamboo Island in a Phi Phi Island yacht charter itinerary gives guests an opportunity to enjoy a quieter beach stop while exploring several destinations during the same journey.

Bamboo Island
Scenic Yacht Anchorage

Bamboo Island’s location near the Phi Phi Islands makes it a convenient stop for private yacht itineraries exploring this part of the Andaman Sea. Guests can enjoy swimming, snorkeling, relaxing on board, or taking in views of the surrounding coastline.

The island can be combined with other nearby destinations as part of a Phi Phi island-hopping yacht trip, depending on your preferred itinerary and cruising schedule.
