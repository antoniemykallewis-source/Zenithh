Phang Nga Bay Yacht Charter from Phuket

Discover the Highlights of Phang Nga Bay

Phang Nga Bay is one of Thailand’s most distinctive coastal destinations, known for its towering limestone karsts, emerald waters, hidden lagoons, sea caves, and secluded islands. Located between Phuket and the mainland along the Andaman Sea, the bay offers an impressive landscape for sightseeing and island exploration.

A Phang Nga Bay yacht charter provides a flexible way to discover the area, allowing you to cruise between famous landmarks, explore sheltered waterways, and experience some of the bay’s quieter surroundings as part of a private itinerary from Phuket.

Phang Nga Bay
James Bond Island

James Bond Island is one of the best-known landmarks in Phang Nga Bay. The destination became internationally recognized after appearing in The Man with the Golden Gun and is particularly famous for Ko Tapu, the distinctive limestone pinnacle rising from the water.

A yacht charter trip to James Bond Island can be combined with other attractions throughout the bay, making it a natural stop on a wider Phang Nga Bay yacht charter itinerary.

Phang Nga Bay
Koh Panyee

Koh Panyee is a distinctive fishing village built largely on stilts over the water beneath towering limestone cliffs. Visitors can explore its walkways, discover aspects of local island life, browse small shops, and enjoy Thai food while taking in views across the bay.

Including Koh Panyee in a Phang Nga Bay yacht trip adds a cultural experience to an itinerary otherwise dominated by the region’s spectacular natural scenery.

Phang Nga Bay
Hong Island

Hong Island in Phang Nga Bay is known for its sheltered lagoon surrounded by dramatic limestone formations. Depending on tides and local conditions, the lagoon and surrounding waterways can be explored by kayak for a closer look at the bay’s distinctive landscape.

A yacht charter trip to Hong Island can be incorporated into a wider journey through Phang Nga Bay, combining scenic cruising with kayaking and exploration.

Phang Nga Bay
Koh Panak

Koh Panak is known for its limestone caves, hidden lagoons, mangrove areas, and unusual rock formations. Kayaking is a popular way to explore selected waterways and caves when tidal and weather conditions permit.

Koh Panak makes a natural addition to a Phang Nga Bay yacht charter itinerary, particularly for guests interested in exploring the quieter and more adventurous side of the bay.

Phang Nga Bay
Koh Hong (Phang Nga)

Not to be confused with the Hong Islands near Krabi, Koh Hong in Phang Nga Bay offers another opportunity to experience the area’s limestone scenery and sheltered waters.

Depending on the selected route and conditions, the island can be incorporated into a private yacht charter in Phang Nga Bay alongside destinations such as James Bond Island, Koh Panak, and Koh Panyee.

Explore Phang Nga Bay by Yacht

From James Bond Island and Koh Panyee to hidden lagoons, sea caves, and towering limestone formations, a Phang Nga Bay yacht charter offers a memorable way to explore one of southern Thailand’s most distinctive coastal landscapes.

A private yacht charter from Phuket to Phang Nga Bay provides the flexibility to combine sightseeing, kayaking, island exploration, and relaxing on board according to your preferred itinerary.
