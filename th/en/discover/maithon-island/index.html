Maithon Island Yacht Charter from Phuket

Discover the Highlights of Maithon Island

Maithon Island is a peaceful tropical destination located off the southeast coast of Phuket, known for its clear waters, sandy shoreline, coral reefs, and relaxed surroundings. Accessible by sea, the island is a popular destination for swimming, snorkeling, and enjoying a quieter day on the Andaman Sea.

A Maithon Island yacht charter offers a comfortable way to explore the island while combining snorkeling, swimming, water activities, and time relaxing on board. Its location also makes it a convenient addition to a Phuket island-hopping yacht trip.

Maithon Island
Pristine White-Sand Beach

Maithon Island features a beautiful sandy shoreline bordered by clear turquoise waters and tropical scenery. The peaceful surroundings provide an inviting setting for relaxing by the beach, swimming, or simply enjoying views across the Andaman Sea.

Visiting as part of a private yacht charter to Maithon Island allows you to combine beach time with other activities during your day at sea.

Maithon Island
Snorkeling & Coral Reefs

The waters surrounding Maithon Island feature coral reefs and a variety of tropical marine life, making snorkeling one of the island’s popular activities. Clear-water conditions can provide good opportunities to observe colorful fish and underwater scenery.

A Maithon Island yacht charter from Phuket allows you to combine snorkeling with swimming, island exploration, and relaxing on board as part of a flexible itinerary.

Maithon Island
Dolphin Spotting

Wild dolphins are occasionally seen in the waters around Maithon Island, adding another memorable possibility to the journey. As these are wild animals in their natural environment, sightings vary and cannot be guaranteed.

A Maithon Island yacht trip also provides plenty to enjoy without a dolphin sighting, from coastal scenery and snorkeling to relaxing on the surrounding waters.

Maithon Island
Crystal-Clear Waters

The clear waters around Maithon Island provide opportunities for swimming, paddleboarding, snorkeling, and other water activities when weather and sea conditions are suitable.

These activities make the island a natural addition to a Phuket yacht charter itinerary for couples, families, and groups looking to combine relaxation with time in the water.

Maithon Island
Exclusive Island Escape

Away from Phuket’s main coastline, Maithon Island provides a relaxing setting for visitors looking to spend a day surrounded by tropical scenery and the Andaman Sea.

Whether you are planning a romantic getaway, family outing, or day with friends, a private yacht charter in Phuket provides the flexibility to incorporate Maithon Island into an itinerary suited to your group.
