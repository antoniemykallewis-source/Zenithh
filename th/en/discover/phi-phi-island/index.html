Phi Phi Island Yacht Charter from Phuket

Discover the Highlights of Phi Phi Island

Phi Phi Island is one of Thailand’s most iconic tropical destinations, known for its turquoise waters, dramatic limestone cliffs, white-sand beaches, and vibrant marine life. Located in the Andaman Sea, the islands are a popular destination for Phuket island hopping by yacht, offering a memorable way to explore secluded bays, lagoons, beaches, and spectacular coastal scenery.

A yacht charter to Phi Phi Island gives you the freedom to discover its famous attractions from the water while enjoying a more comfortable and flexible journey between each destination.

Phi Phi Island
Maya Bay

One of the most famous attractions in the Phi Phi Islands, Maya Bay is surrounded by towering limestone cliffs, crystal-clear waters, and powdery white sand. Made internationally famous by the film The Beach, the bay remains one of Thailand’s most recognizable coastal landscapes.

Visiting Maya Bay as part of a Phi Phi Island yacht trip allows you to experience the spectacular scenery while exploring the surrounding islands and bays by sea.

Phi Phi Island
Pileh Lagoon

Often called the Emerald Lagoon, Pileh Lagoon is a spectacular natural lagoon enclosed by steep limestone cliffs. Its calm turquoise waters and sheltered setting make it a popular stop for swimming, sightseeing, and photography during a Phi Phi Island yacht charter.

Arriving by yacht also provides an unforgettable perspective of the limestone formations surrounding the lagoon.

Phi Phi Island
Monkey Beach

Monkey Beach combines a beautiful stretch of coastline with the chance to observe the area’s wild monkeys from a respectful distance. Surrounded by clear waters and lush limestone scenery, it is another popular stop when exploring Phi Phi Island by yacht.

The surrounding waters and coastline also make Monkey Beach a scenic addition to a Phuket island-hopping yacht trip.

Phi Phi Island
Viking Cave

Located beneath towering limestone cliffs, Viking Cave is one of Phi Phi Island’s distinctive landmarks. The cave is known for paintings found on its interior walls as well as the long-established collection of edible bird’s nests.

Although access to the cave itself is restricted, visitors can admire its dramatic entrance and surrounding cliffs while cruising around Phi Phi Island.

Phi Phi Island
Bamboo Island

Just a short distance from Phi Phi Island, Bamboo Island is a tropical paradise surrounded by coral reefs and pristine beaches. Its peaceful atmosphere makes it perfect for sunbathing, swimming, and snorkeling.
