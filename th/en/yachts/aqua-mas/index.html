Aqua Mas

MotorYacht · 46 ft · Up to 8 guests

Your private yacht in Thailand

Step aboard Aqua Mas.

AQUA MAS is a 46ft Azimut motor yacht in Phuket offering three stylish cabins and private cruising for up to 8 guests. Designed for intimate escapes, she combines comfortable indoor spaces, open-air relaxation and a fully customizable itinerary for exploring Thailand’s islands in privacy.
Private charters start from THB 63,000 for a 5-hour Koh Yao and Naka Island cruise, with onboard amenities including air conditioning, WiFi, paddleboards, snorkeling equipment and a tender boat.
