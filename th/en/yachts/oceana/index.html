Oceana

MotorYacht · 65 ft · Up to 16 guests

Your private yacht in Thailand

Step aboard Oceana.

OCEANA is a 65ft Princess motor yacht available for private yacht charter in Phuket, accommodating up to 16 guests (excluding captain and crew). Featuring four cabins, a spacious saloon, fully equipped kitchen, expansive outdoor areas, and a large flybridge, Oceana is designed for comfortable day cruises and overnight journeys around the Andaman Sea.
Choose from half-day, full-day, and overnight charters to destinations including Khai Islands, Phang Nga Bay, Phi Phi Islands, Racha Yai, Racha Noi, Maithon Island, Naka Island, and other cruising areas around Phuket. Onboard amenities include air conditioning, Wi-Fi, Netflix, paddleboards, snorkeling equipment, fishing rods, towable water toys, a tender boat, and refreshments.
