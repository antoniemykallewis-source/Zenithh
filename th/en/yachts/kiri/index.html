Your private yacht in Thailand

Step aboard Kiri.

KIRI is a traditional Thai longtail boat available for private charter in Phuket, accommodating up to 8 guests (excluding captain and crew). With an open-air layout and a low-to-the-water cruising experience, Kiri offers a relaxed way for couples, families, and small groups to explore Phuket’s nearby islands and coastline.
Choose from private half-day and full-day trips to destinations including Coral Island, Lon Island, Maithon Island, Khai Islands, and Promthep Cape. Selected itineraries combine snorkeling, beach stops, paddleboarding, swimming, and sunset views for a distinctly local Phuket boating experience.
