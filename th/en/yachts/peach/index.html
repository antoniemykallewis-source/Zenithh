Peach

Sailing Catamaran · 42 ft · Up to 35 guests

Your private yacht in Thailand

Step aboard Peach.

PEACH is a 42ft Lagoon sailing catamaran available for private yacht charter in Phuket, offering half-day, full-day, and sunset charters around the Andaman Sea. With spacious outdoor decks and comfortable lounge areas, Peach is well suited to families, groups of friends, celebrations, and relaxed island-hopping trips.
Choose from itineraries to Coral Island, Maithon Island, Racha Yai, Racha Noi, Khai Islands, and Phi Phi Islands, or enjoy an afternoon and sunset cruise around Promthep Cape. Onboard equipment includes a paddleboard, kayak, clear kayak, fishing rods, towels, and a dinghy, with optional air conditioning, water slide, and inflatable pool available.
