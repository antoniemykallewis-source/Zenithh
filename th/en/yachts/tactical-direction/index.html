Tactical Direction

Sailing Catamaran · 47 ft · Up to 35 guests

Your private yacht in Thailand

Step aboard Tactical Direction.

TACTICAL DIRECTION is a 47ft sailing catamaran available for private yacht charter in Phuket, accommodating up to 35 guests (excluding captain and crew). Based at Chalong Pier, its spacious layout and large guest capacity make it well suited to families, groups, celebrations, and private island-hopping trips.
Choose from half-day, full-day, and sunset charter itineraries to Coral Island, Maithon Island, Racha Yai, Racha Noi, Khai Islands, Phi Phi Island, and Promthep Cape. Onboard equipment includes a kayak or paddleboard, snorkeling masks, floats, and handline fishing equipment for a relaxed day exploring Phuket’s surrounding islands.
