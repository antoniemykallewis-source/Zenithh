Myra

Sailing Catamaran · 47 ft · Up to 30 guests

Your private yacht in Thailand

Step aboard Myra.

MYRA is a 47ft Leopard sailing catamaran available for private yacht charter in Phuket, accommodating up to 30 guests (excluding captain and crew). With generous deck space, comfortable indoor and outdoor areas, and air conditioning, Myra is well suited to group outings, celebrations, full-day island trips, and overnight cruising around the Andaman Sea.
Choose from full-day charters to Phang Nga Bay, Khai and Naka Islands, or Koh Hong and Krabi, as well as 2D1N and 3D2N overnight journeys. Onboard amenities include a sea scooter, paddleboard, swim float, snorkeling equipment, beach towels, refreshments, and air conditioning, with additional water activities available.
