How Much Does a Yacht Charter Cost in Singapore?

Yacht charter pricing varies depending on a few key factors. Understanding these will help you choose the ideal vessel for your budget and group size.

1. Duration and Charter Type
Short Day Charters (4–8 Hours): The most popular option for casual outings, birthday celebrations, or sunset cruises. Prices generally range from SGD 500 to SGD 4,000+, depending on the size and luxury level of the vessel.

Overnight and Multi-Day Charters: Perfect for weekend getaways to neighboring islands. Longer rentals start around SGD 4,000 and can reach SGD 30,000+ for premium superyachts.

2. Vessel Size and Style
Mid-Sized Cruisers (40–50 ft): Ideal for smaller groups, family days out, or casual fishing trips. These offer great value without compromising comfort.

Luxury Catamarans & Superyachts: Larger vessels, like a 108-foot superyacht or wide-beam catamaran, offer expansive entertainment decks, indoor lounges, fully equipped kitchens, and luxury cabins. Higher charter fees reflect premium onboard features such as jacuzzis, high-end sound systems, and karaoke setups.

3. Inclusions vs. Add-Ons
Base charter fees typically cover the yacht rental, captain, crew, fuel, and standard safety gear. To elevate your event, charter companies offer custom add-ons, including:

Gourmet catering or private chef services

  • Water sports gear (jet skis, sea scooters, paddleboards, and inflatable slides)
  • Event decor for birthdays, proposals, or corporate functions
  • Why Chartering is the Ultimate Way to Enjoy the Sea

4.Zero Logistics, Maximum Relaxation
When you charter, professional captains and crews handle every single detail—from maritime compliance and route planning to safety checks and hospitality. All you need to do is step aboard, kick back, and enjoy the trip.

5.Variety and Flexibility
Chartering gives you the freedom to choose a different vessel for every event. Host a casual weekend BBQ on a cozy catamaran one month, and book a sleek motor yacht for a black-tie corporate event the next. You get access to a full fleet tailored specifically to your group size and vibe.

6.Unbeatable Value
Instead of absorbing ongoing expenses like marina berths, insurance, and routine maintenance, chartering ensures you pay strictly for the time you spend on the water. It’s an efficient, cost-effective way to enjoy a high-end experience without long-term overhead.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I host private events or proposals on board?

Absolutely! Private charters offer tailored packages for proposals, birthdays, corporate retreats, and micro-weddings. You can customize everything from floral arrangements and live acoustic sets to private dining menus.

What is typically included in a charter package?

Standard charters include the yacht, captain, crew, fuel for designated routes, and basic water amenities (like kayaks or floaties). Food, alcoholic beverages, and specialized water toys can usually be added to your package upon request.

How far in advance should I book?

For weekend slots, public holidays, and peak festive periods (such as November and December), it is best to book 4 to 6 weeks in advance. Early booking ensures you secure your preferred vessel and timing.
