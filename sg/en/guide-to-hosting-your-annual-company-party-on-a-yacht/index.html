A Guide to Hosting Your Annual Company Party on a Yacht

Key Takeaways

  • A yacht offers a refreshing way to host your annual company party, giving your team sea views, open space, and a break from the usual ballrooms.
  • Customisable itineraries and onboard amenities—from water toys to curated catering—let you shape the celebration to fit your company culture.
  • Extra touches, like professional photography, help capture memories your team can share long after the event.
  • Ending with a thoughtful gesture, such as a toast or branded treat, makes your corporate event both meaningful and memorable.

Why Your Next Company Party Belongs on the Water

Forget predictable hotel buffets and recycled party themes—your team deserves more! Swap out the traditional function room for a yacht, and let your annual company party unfold against Singapore’s open waters. Here’s a guide to help you plan your next big staff appreciation event with Zenith Yacht Charters.

1. Choose the Right Yacht for Your Team Size

The first step to planning your annual company party is finding a yacht that matches your team size. A yacht like the Sunrise, for instance, comfortably hosts up to 18 guests—perfect for smaller teams who want a cosy setting with plenty of room to mingle and relax. For larger groups, the Leviathan 8 yacht is a standout choice, accommodating up to 55 people with its spacious flybridge and amenities such as a jacuzzi, a fully stocked bar, and a flexible bimini. If you’re planning for more than 55 guests, simply contact us for recommendations on suitable arrangements. With our diverse fleet, Zenith Yacht Charters makes it easy to scale your event to suit your needs.

2. Customise the Experience to Match Your Company Culture

No two teams celebrate the same way. Maybe your crew loves firing up the grill, cracking open beers, and keeping things laid-back. Or maybe you’re after something sleeker—cocktails at sunset, a live DJ, and a touch of glamour. Whatever your style, Zenith brings your annual company party to life in a way that reflects your workplace vibe. Choose from a range of add-ons, from extra water toys like jet skis and kayaks for adventurous teams to upgraded amenities that vary across different vessels. It’s your culture, your rules—just with a better view.

3. Plan an Itinerary That Balances Fun and Flow

Nobody wants a company gathering that feels like another day at work. Instead, think of your itinerary as a rhythm: light cruising to start, team games or water activities to get people laughing, and then a big group toast as the sky shifts from gold to deep blue. With our customisable itineraries, you can choose whether to sail out to the Southern Islands, anchor for a swim, or keep things simple with a relaxing cruise—striking the balance that works best for your team.

4. Capture the Moments That Matter

Hosting a company retreat on a private boat is more than just an event—it’s a memory worth keeping. Capture candid shots of colleagues laughing on deck, raising a toast at sunset, or diving into the water during playtime. Many of Zenith’s yachts also come with high-quality sound systems, so you can create a lively atmosphere that looks as good on camera as it feels in person. For added convenience, we can even arrange a professional photographer or videographer for a fee, ensuring your celebration is preserved in stunning detail.

5. End on a High Note with a Thoughtful Gesture

A party feels even more meaningful when it ends with something personal. Wrap up your annual company party with a short speech of appreciation, a toast from leadership, or even small tokens of thanks for employees. Our yacht dining services can also include customised desserts or branded treats that tie back to your company. It’s a simple way to remind your team that the event isn’t just about fun—it’s about recognising their contributions and building stronger bonds.

Why Take the Party Offshore?

At the end of the day, your annual company party is all about showing your team that their hard work matters. Corporate yacht charters give you the freedom to celebrate in style—without feeling boxed in by four walls. Whether it’s an intimate cruise or a lively bash aboard, the combination of sea, sky, and shared memories makes for an event that feels fresh, fun, and totally different from the usual routine.

With Zenith Yacht Charters, hosting a unique company retreat is both seamless and surprisingly affordable. Our fleet offers plenty of options for different group sizes, while our team handles the planning details so you don’t have to. From catering to add-ons, everything is arranged to make your event effortless for you—and unforgettable for your team.
