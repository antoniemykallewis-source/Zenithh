Exploring the World of Fishing Charters in Singapore

Nestled within the embrace of surrounding waters on all sides, Singapore’s connection to the sea runs deep. Consider our early days as a fishing village and rich folklore that point back to fish – like the attack of the swordfish from which Redhill got its name and the mythical Merlion.

Despite our rich maritime heritage, regulations prohibit fishing at various parks and canals. To beat the crowd at designated fishing grounds, get a fishing charter to bring you closer to your catch.

What is a fishing charter?

A fishing charter brings you and your kakis on an expedition with fishing guides, equipment, and expertise in navigating local waters. Whether focusing on offshore or inshore fishing, fishing charters cater to both novice and experienced anglers for an impressive haul.

Casting the net on the ideal fishing charters in Singapore

Any boat can take you to fishing grounds. A proper fishing charter will equip you with the right equipment and expertise to reel in a catch. If you are on the lookout for the right boat, here are some things to keep in mind.

An Experienced Fishing Captain

A seasoned fishing captain will have an intimate knowledge of the waters and the fish that lie within. Their expertise becomes the most trustworthy compass to guide anglers to the elusive, larger catches. More experienced than fishing guides, captains can increase the probability of a bountiful and rewarding expedition.

Shelter from the elements

Fishing is for the patient. It can take hours to hook on to something you are proud of. In our tropical climate, the sun and rain are sure to accompany you on your wait. Get a fishing charter with a sheltered deck and cabin areas to keep you well-protected throughout your trip.

Clear navigation

With an in-built GPS system, fishing charters can bring you to prime fishing locations in Singapore without the guesswork. Expect precise navigation that also makes it easy to navigate back to the harbour in case of emergencies.

Fish finders

Make waves on your fishing expedition when you can pinpoint optimal spots for casting lines. Fish finders use sonar technology to track schools of fish beyond where your eyes can see. With the right technology, you can optimise fishing times and increase the likelihood of bragging rights.

Bait well

Everyone wants the freshest ingredients – and that includes the fish you are trying to lure in. Bait wells create a mini aquarium onboard your fishing charter to keep your bait alive and fresh before they get hooked and plunged into the deep blue.
