Fun Yacht Activities You Need to Try

Key Takeaways

  • A yacht charter in Singapore offers more than sailing—it’s about creating memorable experiences through a selection of interesting yacht activities.
  • Cool off in Singapore’s open waters near Lazarus or St. John’s Island for an adventurous way to connect with nature.
  • Unwind on floating mats, paddleboards, or inflatables for a calm and carefree break from city life.
  • Turn the deck into your personal dance floor with music, themed outfits, and sea breezes for a night to remember.
  • Time your charter for golden hour and soak in panoramic skyline views, perfect for romance and stunning photos.
  • Elevate your dinner plans with BBQ, catering, or a private chef while surrounded by the ocean’s beauty.

5 Interesting Things to Do on a Yacht in Singapore

When it comes to planning a memorable day out, a yacht charter in Singapore opens doors to unique experiences you won’t find anywhere else. Whether you’re looking to celebrate with friends, spend quality time with family, or enjoy a romantic escape with your significant other, there are endless yacht activities you can explore.

There’s nothing quite like cooling off in the open sea whilst on a private yacht. Take a trip to the Southern Islands, such as Lazarus or St. John’s, and dive straight into their inviting waters. With the crew on hand to ensure safety, both kids and adults can enjoy a refreshing swim or a leisurely snorkel. It’s a simple yet exhilarating way to embrace the ocean and add a splash of adventure to your day.

Option 1: Float and Relax on Water Toys

Not in the mood for high-energy fun? Take it easy with floating mats, paddleboards, or inflatables provided onboard. These yacht activities are perfect for both kids and adults, whether you want to lounge under the sun, drift gently on the waves, or snap a few Instagram-worthy photos. Plus, floating on open water offers a rare sense of calm and escape—offering a welcome contrast to the bustle of city life.

Option 2: Host a Mini Dance Party Onboard

If you’re chartering for a celebration, few yacht activities are as fun as turning the deck into your own private dance floor. Load up your playlist, connect your speakers, and let the music set the mood. To make it even more memorable, set a themed dress code or bring along glow-in-the-dark accessories to light up the night. Whether under the glow of the sunset or beneath a starry sky, let yourself move to the music with the sea breeze all around. It’s the finishing touch that makes your yacht party truly unforgettable.

Option 3: Enjoy a Scenic Sunset Cruise

Few experiences rival the beauty of watching the sky change colours while out at sea. Time your yacht rental in Singapore to coincide with golden hour, and you’ll be treated to sweeping views as the skyline glows against the setting sun. For couples, it sets the stage for a romantic, intimate moment, while groups will appreciate the chance to capture stunning photos together. A sunset cruise is an excellent choice if you’re looking for some light entertainment on your yacht trip, delivering an experience that lingers long after you’ve returned to shore.

Option 4: Feast With a View

What could be better than enjoying a meal with the sea as your backdrop? With Zenith Yacht Charters, you can choose from a casual BBQ, a fully catered spread, or even a private chef to elevate the experience. Picture yourself savouring fresh seafood as the Marina Bay skyline glitters in the distance or as Sentosa’s coastline drifts by. No matter the menu, a dinner on a yacht pairs delicious flavours with breathtaking views, creating a dining moment you’ll always remember.
