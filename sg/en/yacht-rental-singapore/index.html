Discover Your Ideal Yacht: Types of Yachts

The world of yacht rentals offers a plethora of choices. Whether you’re an avid sailor seeking the thrill of the open sea, a luxury enthusiast in pursuit of comfort, or an angler eager to reel in your next trophy catch, there are yacht rental options perfectly suited to your adventure.

We delve into the features, benefits, and unique experiences that each type of vessel brings. The following guide will help you navigate the rich seas of yacht rentals, ensuring your maritime adventure is precisely tailored to you.

Monohull Yacht

Monohull yachts are the classic, single-hulled sailboats renowned for their timeless elegance and versatility. These yachts are celebrated for their graceful lines and efficient design, offering a splendid sailing experience that connects enthusiasts with the elemental beauty of wind and sea.

Sailing Catamaran

Catamarans, in contrast to monohulls, feature twin hulls and are known for their exceptional stability, spaciousness, and smooth sailing experience. Sailing catamarans have a wider deck space, which allows for more outdoor seating and recreational areas.

This design provides an open and inviting atmosphere for guests. The stability of catamarans makes them an excellent choice for those who might be prone to seasickness or simply prefer a more stable ride. Sailing catamarans offer the best of both worlds, combining the thrill of sailing with the comfort of a stable platform, and they are an excellent choice for both beginners and seasoned sailors.

Power Catamaran

Power catamarans share the twin-hulled design with sailing catamarans but are equipped with powerful engines for added speed and convenience. This combination of stability and propulsion creates a versatile vessel that’s perfect for those seeking a smooth and swift cruising experience.

Power catamarans offer ample deck space, providing comfort for leisurely voyages and island hopping. They are well-suited for exploring coastal destinations with comfortable cabins for your comfort. With the added speed of engines, you can cover more ground in less time, allowing for extended exploration of beautiful coastal regions and remote islands.

Fishing Boats

Fishing boats come in a wide range of sizes and designs, each catering to the specific needs and preferences of anglers. From small recreational fishing boats equipped with basic fishing gear to larger vessels designed for deep-sea angling adventures, fishing boats offer anglers the perfect platform for pursuing their passion.

Super Yacht

Super Yachts represent the pinnacle of luxury and opulence on the water. The Leviathan 8 is a 105ft Horizon Super Yacht, perfect for hosting events. Gather up to 55 pax and split your party between the spacious salon, 2 cabins with ensuite bathrooms, an expansive aft deck for al fresco dining, a flybridge with a Jacuzzi and sun pads, and a forward seating area with breathtaking views. Enjoy easy water access from the spacious swim platform and indulge in water sports with included amenities like a kayak and stand up paddle board.
