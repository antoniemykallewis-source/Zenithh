How to Plan a Yacht-Based Wedding Celebration in Singapore

Every little girl has dreamed of her wedding with Pinterest boards for the perfect dress, hairdo, decor and ring. On our little island, it can be hard to secure a venue that lives up to your Pinterest board dreams. You could have a destination wedding but that makes it difficult for all your guests to attend. Luckily, there’s a middle ground. Have a luxury yacht wedding in Singapore and make the open seas your ‘something blue’.

Gather the Building Blocks For a Yacht Wedding

Eat, Drink and Be Merry

Forget the traditional banquet-style dining where both your stomach and belt can’t get a spoon of fried rice through by the end. Dining on a yacht doesn’t get more premium than this. We are talking about a 1-Michelin-Star chef cooking for you and your guests. Slurp on sashimi-grade Japanese oysters as you hear the wagyu tomahawk sizzling on the live grill. Let us know the dietary needs of your party and we will curate a menu that will leave your guests taking notes for their big day.

Flow to the Rhythm of Love

Make sure you have waterproof mascara on because the live band might just play your couple song. We can arrange for live music and a DJ to play a romantic set list. Talent doesn’t run in every family so let your guests take a back seat and enjoy the party while the professionals take hold of the mic.

Capture your ‘I Do’ in High Definition

It’s easy to get so lost in the hosting that you forget to take it all in. Add on photography and videography so you can soak in your wedding festivities and look back at the happiest day of your life. Love won’t be the only thing in the air. With the option for drone videography, capture the panoramic view of the seas and celebration from every angle.

Tying the Knot in Style

Yacht wedding decor does not have to be any less impressive than on land. Depending on your budget and yacht selection, adorn the space with floral arches, table arrangements, a reception table set up with photos, fairy lights on outdoor railings and more! Let your fairytale wedding come alive on the sea.
