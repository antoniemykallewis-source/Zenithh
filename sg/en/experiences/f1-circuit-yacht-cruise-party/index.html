F1 Circuit Yacht Cruise Party in Singapore

F1 SINGAPORE GRAND PRIX BY YACHT

THE ULTIMATE RACEFRONT EXPERIENCE

Experience the thrill of Singapore’s F1 weekend like never before. From your yacht charter at Marina Bay, watch the race live on premium screens while feeling the roar of the cars echoing from the track.

With the city skyline glowing around you, enjoy light refreshments, curated bites, and the perfect blend of high-energy racing and serene luxury – a truly unforgettable way to celebrate the weekend.

FIREWORKS OVER MARINA BAY

A SKY PAINTED IN CELEBRATION

As the night unfolds, the sky above Marina Bay lights up in a spectacular display of fireworks. From your private deck, witness the colours reflecting on the water, creating a breathtaking panorama that blends perfectly with the excitement of the race.

Give a toast to your entourage as each spark marks a moment of celebration, turning your evening into a magical and unforgettable experience.

MARINA BAY SANDS NIGHTSCAPE

THE CITY ILLUMINATED IN ELEGANCE

Take in the iconic Marina Bay Sands skyline as it shimmers against the calm waters. The glowing city lights, mirrored on the bay, create an elegant and serene backdrop for your evening.

Relax on deck, soak in the views, and enjoy the perfect combination of luxury, excitement, and the beauty of Singapore by night – an experience that captures the essence of a truly unforgettable F1 weekend.
