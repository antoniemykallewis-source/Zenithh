Frequently Asked Questions

What types of fishing trips are available with a fishing boat charter?

With our fishing boat charter in Singapore, you can enjoy coastal fishing, full-day offshore trips, catch-and-cook experiences, or even cross-border adventures, all fully customisable. Whether you’re looking to stay close to shore or venture further into open waters, we’ve got something for every thrill-seeker. Explore our experiences or contact us to plan your next fishing getaway.

Do I need prior fishing experience to rent a fishing yacht?

Not at all! Here at Zenith Yacht Charters, our fishing yacht rentals in Singapore welcome everyone, from beginners to seasoned anglers. Our experienced crew is there to assist, providing guidance and equipment to ensure a successful and enjoyable experience.

What equipment is included with a fishing yacht rental?

Our fishing boat rentals in Singapore come fully equipped with essential fishing gear, bait, and tackle. For an even more exciting experience, check out our add-ons to enhance your adventure. You’re welcome to bring any personal gear as well.

Can I bring my own fishing equipment?

Absolutely! Many of our guests, especially experienced anglers bring their own gear. Still, we provide well-maintained, high-quality fishing equipment should you wish to travel light. We do recommend keeping personal gear to a reasonable amount (typically up to four rods), as bringing too many may make it harder for our crew to assist and could result in tangled lines. For newer anglers, we suggest using the gear provided onboard to ensure a smooth, enjoyable trip. View all experiences or get in touch to tailor your trip.

How many people can join a fishing yacht rental?

Depending on the vessel, our fishing yacht rentals in Singapore can comfortably accommodate groups of various sizes. Let us know your group size, and we’ll help select the perfect yacht for you.

What if we don’t catch any fish?

While we can’t guarantee a catch every time, our crew is familiar with the best fishing spots around Singapore. Our goal is to provide a memorable experience on the water, whether it’s a bountiful catch or simply a scenic, relaxing day on a fishing boat rental in Singapore.
