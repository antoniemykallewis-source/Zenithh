How can I plan a surprise yacht proposal in Singapore?

What is the capacity of your yachts for a wedding?

Our fleet offers a range of yachts with varying capacities, from intimate gatherings to larger celebrations. Depending on your preferred yacht, we can accommodate both small, private ceremonies and more spacious events.

Do you offer packages for a boat wedding in Singapore?

Absolutely. Our bespoke wedding experiences cover both yacht and boat settings, giving you flexibility to choose the style and atmosphere that best suits your celebration.

How far in advance should I book my yacht wedding in Singapore?

To secure your preferred yacht and date, we recommend booking at least three to six months in advance. Early planning also gives our team more time to perfect every detail. Contact us to share your plans.

What is included in a yacht wedding package in Singapore?

A yacht wedding package typically includes venue rental, basic décor, sound system setup, and crew services. Many packages can also be enhanced with catering, floral arrangements, entertainment, and photography, depending on your preferences. The inclusions may vary, so it is best to review the details and customise them to suit your event.

Can I customise my boat wedding setup and decorations?

Yes, most providers offer flexible options to customise your boat wedding setup. You can personalise elements such as décor themes, seating arrangements, catering menus, and entertainment to match your vision. This allows you to create celebrations that reflect your style while making the most of the yacht setting.

Browse our fleet of yachts to find one that best suits your guest size, layout preferences, and overall wedding concept. From intimate vessels for smaller gatherings to larger yachts designed for celebrations, each option offers different features and capacities to support your ideal setup.

Is a boat wedding suitable for small or intimate ceremonies in Singapore?

A boat wedding is well-suited for small and intimate ceremonies. Yachts provide a private and cosy setting, making them ideal for solemnisation or close-knit celebrations with family and friends. The controlled guest size also allows for a more meaningful and personalised experience. Contact our team to discover how you can plan for your very own memorable yacht experience.
