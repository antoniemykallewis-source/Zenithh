Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a corporate yacht charter ideal for team-building events?

A corporate yacht charter offers a unique environment that fosters team bonding and collaboration. Our charters provide a relaxed, scenic setting that’s perfect for building connections outside the office.

How many people can a corporate yacht accommodate?

Our fleet includes various sizes of yachts suitable for corporate gatherings, so we can easily accommodate both small and large groups. Just let us know your group size, and we’ll recommend the ideal corporate yacht charter option.

Can I customise the experience for my team?

Absolutely! We specialise in creating tailored experiences for corporate clients, offering activities and amenities that can be customised to fit your team’s goals and preferences.

Are food and drinks available on corporate charters?

Yes, we offer a range of catering options for corporate charters. Whether you need snacks and refreshments or a full meal, we’ll arrange food and drinks to suit your event needs.

What happens if the weather is unfavourable?

In Singapore, light drizzles are common and often pass within a short period. Under most circumstances, the charter will proceed as scheduled.

If it is raining at the scheduled departure time, guests are kindly requested to proceed to the designated meeting point and await further instructions from the captain. The captain will assess the weather and sea conditions on the day and has sole discretion to determine whether it is safe to proceed. In cases of heavy rain, the departure may be delayed until conditions improve.

Should weather conditions be deemed unsafe for the charter to operate, we will work with you to arrange a mutually agreeable postponement date.

Is prior booking necessary for corporate yacht charters?

We recommend booking in advance to ensure the best availability, especially for large groups. Early yacht booking also allows us to tailor your experience for maximum impact.
