Frequently Asked Questions

What types of yachts are available for rent with Zenith Yacht Charters?

Zenith Yacht Charters offers a carefully curated fleet, ranging from private yachts perfect for intimate celebrations to spacious vessels designed for parties and corporate events. Each yacht is equipped with modern amenities to elevate your experience. Explore our yacht rental options to find the perfect match for your occasion.

How far in advance should I book a yacht?

To ensure availability, we recommend booking your yacht charter Singapore at least two to four weeks in advance, especially during peak seasons. Early bookings not only secure your preferred yacht but also provide ample time for our team to personalise your experience. Learn more about the different experiences here.

Can I rent a yacht for just a few hours?

Yes, we offer flexible rental durations, allowing you to rent a yacht for as little as a few hours or enjoy a full day at sea. Whether you’re planning a quick getaway or a luxurious day trip, our yacht rental Singapore services are tailored to fit your schedule. Check out our services page for more details.

Are food and drinks allowed onboard?

Yes! You can bring your own refreshments or let us arrange catering for a hassle-free experience. Many of our clients choose customised food and drink packages to enhance their private yacht event. Explore our food and beverage options for a truly memorable experience.

What if the weather is bad on the day of my rental?

In Singapore, light drizzles are common and often pass within a short period. Under most circumstances, the charter will proceed as scheduled.

If it is raining at the scheduled departure time, guests are kindly requested to proceed to the designated meeting point and await further instructions from the captain. The captain will assess the weather and sea conditions on the day and has sole discretion to determine whether it is safe to proceed. In cases of heavy rain, the departure may be delayed until conditions improve.

Should weather conditions be deemed unsafe for the charter to operate, we will work with you to arrange a mutually agreeable postponement date.

Do I need a licence to rent a boat?

No, a licence is not required when booking a crewed yacht charter with Zenith Yacht. Our professional crew will handle all aspects of navigation, allowing you to relax and fully enjoy your experience.
