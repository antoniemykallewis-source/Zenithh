Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in your yacht dining experience in Singapore?

Our yacht dining experience in Singapore usually includes a private charter, a thoughtfully arranged dining set-up, tableware, and crew support throughout your time on board. Guests may select curated menus or bring their own dishes if they prefer. If you would like something customised, feel free to contact us and share your ideas.

Can I book a private yacht dinner in Singapore just for two people or a small group?

Yes, you can. A private yacht dinner in Singapore is often booked by couples who want a romantic evening as well as by small groups celebrating special occasions. Here at Zenith Yacht Charters, we can shape the ambience, menu and timing to fit the style of experience you want.

How many guests can be accommodated for a dinner on a yacht in Singapore?

Capacity varies depending on the vessel. Some yachts are designed for intimate settings, while others can host slightly larger gatherings comfortably. If you want to know which option suits your group size, simply contact us and our team will assist you.

What sightseeing or views can we expect during the yacht dining cruise in Singapore?

A dinner on a yacht in Singapore often includes views of the Marina coastline, the city skyline, and the calm waters surrounding the Southern Islands. The shift from daylight to sunset and the evening glow adds a beautiful atmosphere to the dining experience.

Where is the boarding point and what time should we arrive for the private yacht dinner in Singapore?

Boarding is usually at marinas such as ONE°15 Marina or Marina at Keppel Bay, depending on your booking. Arriving 15 minutes before or a little earlier will help you settle in before departure. Once your reservation is confirmed, our team will share exact details, or you can contact us anytime for more information.
