Your private yacht in Singapore

Step aboard MnF.

MnF is a 51ft Majesty monohull. The interior of the yacht has a spacious salon area, 3 cabins and 2 bathrooms. The fully air-conditioned indoor environment, more comfortable for you. The yacht max capacity is 25 pax. In addition to indoors, you can also go to the open flybridge area on the second floor of the yacht, where you will have a better view and enjoy the sun. Moreover, theyacht is also equipped with 4 fishing rods for you to use. The fish you catch can be enjoyed directly on board, as the yacht also prepares BBQ stove for you, and the crew will also help you clean up and cook.
