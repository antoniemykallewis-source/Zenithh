Step aboard Alpha Meta.

A 40ft Cranchi monohull yacht. Currently, it is berthed at One°15 Marina Sentosa Cove. The interior of the yacht is exquisite, neat and clean. The yacht not only have a cabin which passengers can rest, but also a kitchen and bathroom. The flybridge of the yacht is a good place to enjoy the sea view, and you can also take pictures at the front of the yacht.
