Sunreef 62

Sailing Catamaran · 62 ft · Up to 50 guests

Your private yacht in Singapore

Step aboard Sunreef 62.

Sunreef 62 is one of the largest and luxurious of yachts available for charter. This yacht easily hosts entire families, corporate teams. It is even a possible venue hold smaller-scale weddings. At the front of the yacht, you’ll find trampoline netting that spans across the width of the bow – one of the best spots to be to catch that beautiful setting sun in the cooling sea breeze. Inside, you will find a luxurious saloon that leads to multiple cabins. The saloon is fully equipped with hardware to fulfill whatever multimedia requirements your occasion may require. There is also a mini-bar amidst the various kitchen appliances that you will find in the lounge.​
