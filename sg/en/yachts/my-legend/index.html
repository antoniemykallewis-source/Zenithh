MY Legend

Motoryacht · 68 ft · Up to 30 guests

Your private yacht in Singapore

Step aboard MY Legend.

MY Legend is a 68-footer luxury Aquitalia motoryacht best suited for hosting exclusive parties for families, friends and business partners alike. She is a high-end line product with dynamic design, extraordinary craftsmanship, excellent quality and high-end configuration. The gigantic sunbeds on the huge flybridge makes for relaxed lounging with panoramic view of the surroundings. The spacious main salon is ideal for meetings, product launches, as well as intimate celebrations. Each charter comes with a dedicated butler to ensure that guests experience the best hospitality that MY Legend offers.
