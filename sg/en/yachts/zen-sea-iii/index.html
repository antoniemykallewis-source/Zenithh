Zen Sea III

Motoryacht · 55 ft · Up to 24 guests

Your private yacht in Singapore

Step aboard Zen Sea III.

Zen Sea III is an Azimut 55-footer luxury yacht with a flybridge made in Italy. A fast, sporty and modern yacht with practical features, Azimuts are well known for their impeccable finishing and class. She has a huge flybridge that includes sun-bathing areas and a panoramic view of the surroundings. Below decks, she is partitioned with 3 cabins, 2 heads, a huge saloon and a cockpit. She is propelled by Caterpillar twin-engines that are powerful and reliable. Zen Sea 3 is a glamorous and luxurious yacht that offers comfort and excellent sea keeping. She is perfect for family outings, couple getaways, reunions and corporate networking events.
