Step aboard Esora I.

Escape the city and discover the beauty of Singapore’s Southern Islands onboard Esora I, a Beneteau Antares 9.80 designed for comfortable and intimate private yacht charters. Pack your favourite food and drinks, relax on deck, soak up the sunshine, and cruise through Singapore’s sparkling southern waters while taking in stunning island views. Whether you’re celebrating a special occasion, enjoying a family outing, or simply looking for a relaxing getaway with friends, Esora I offers the perfect setting for an unforgettable island-hopping experience.
