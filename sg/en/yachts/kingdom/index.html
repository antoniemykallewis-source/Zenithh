Kingdom

Power Catamaran · 53 ft · Up to 42 guests

Your private yacht in Singapore

Step aboard Kingdom.

Kingdom is a Majestic Custom Built Power Catamaran, licensed to carry up to 42 passengers (before covid) on private cruises around southern islands of Singapore and extended trips to Malaysia, Indonesia or Thailand. Kingdom is equipped with an air-conditioned saloon that can comfortably house up to 8 guests with direct access to the outdoor dining table. The outdoor saloon is a large area that can provide guests a sheltered area for dining, chit-chat and other activities. At the bow of the yacht lies the front deck with bean bags, which is a perfect spot to chill out and enjoy the refreshing sea breeze while sailing.
