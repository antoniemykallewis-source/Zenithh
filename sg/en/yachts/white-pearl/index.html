White Pearl

Motoryacht, Fishing Boats · 43 ft · Up to 13 guests

Your private yacht in Singapore

Step aboard White Pearl.

White Pearl is a 43ft Riviera monohull yacht with a swim platform. The saloon area has a sofa, bed and dining area which is convertible. The yacht is equipped with bluetooth sound system and karaoke, as well as a 55″ TV. In addition, the kitchen is also fully equipped with a microwave oven, induction cooker and gas grill.
