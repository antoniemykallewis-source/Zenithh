Step aboard Golden Ocean.

Golden Ocean boasts a spacious lounge area in its fully air-conditioned saloon deck that can hold up to 10 guests comfortably. Said saloon deck also encompasses an accompanying spacious lounge area. To give it a uniquely lavish atmosphere, Golden Ocean also comes furnished with a set of beautiful furniture and elegant seating features that add to the whole luxury experience while providing comfort when you need it. The boat also comes equipped with multimedia capabilities, ensuring that you will be able to deliver any important presentation; or fulfil any other media requirements you may have.
