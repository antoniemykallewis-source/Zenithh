Your private yacht in Singapore

Step aboard Crazy Monkey.

Crazy Monkey is a majestic Custom Built Monohull,​ licensed to carry up to 30 passengers. The yacht also boasts abundant outdoor space, where sunlight bathes the deck, providing an ideal spot for relaxing in the sea breeze, sunbathing, or chatting on cozy seats. It has a vintage and characterful interiors with a personalized bar where guests can socialize and have fun with friends and family. The entire layout and facilities of the yacht combine comfort and entertainment, making it an ideal choice whether you’re seeking thrilling water activities or simply wishing to relax and unwind on the serene.​
