Step aboard Sardinia.

Named after the second-largest island in the Mediterranean Sea, SARDINIA promises a unique experience – an island getaway that people often associate with the Mediterranean Sea. Standing at 57ft, the three decks found on Sardinia offers plenty of space – ideal for large groups and parties as it is capable of carrying up to 38 passengers comfortably. Immerse yourself in the occasion knowing you will be in the safe hands of the capable boat crew on board. With two bedrooms in the lower deck of the yacht, Sardinia is also ideal for overnight trips allowing for well-deserved rest when needed.​
