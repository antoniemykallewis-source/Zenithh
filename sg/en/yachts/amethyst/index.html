Your private yacht in Singapore

Step aboard Amethyst.

AMETHYST was developed to set a new standard as the state-of-the-art, pure-bred Aquila 48 Power Catamaran with a perfect balance between luxurious comfort and performance. The main deck offers extended spaces for living large, with beautiful panoramic views and access to both forward and aft covered cockpit seating areas. Inside, comfortable saloon seating ensures leisure dining and entertainment. The galley is ergonomically designed with the most modern of appliances, and all the cabins are well lit thanks to their panoramic windows, spacious ensuite bathrooms and showers stalls.
