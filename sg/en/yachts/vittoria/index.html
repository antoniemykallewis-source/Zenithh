Your private yacht in Singapore

Step aboard Vittoria.

A fast, stable and very dry ride with a spacious 3 stateroom layout makes this yacht perfect for daily cruises as well as extended trips. elegant and sporty, her large flybridge is ideal for sunbathing and dining and maximizes exterior space and enjoyment. Ferretti yachts are renowned for their quality, reliability, and comfort and have an excellent reputation within the industry. The interior is luxurious and well-finished throughout, although it is her beautiful exterior lines that will turn heads as she cruises the warm waters of Singapore. Her crew will cater to your every need and ensure an unforgettable day at Lazarus. The spacious aft deck and flybridge are both prime locations from which to enjoy the sun, take a cold drink, or enjoy freshly prepared catered food. Climb aboard today and enjoy an amazing yacht charter experience and make memories that will last for a lifetime!
