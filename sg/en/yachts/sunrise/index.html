SunRise

Sailing Catamaran · 40 ft · Up to 18 guests

Your private yacht in Singapore

Step aboard SunRise.

SunRise is a beautiful 40 feet sailing catamaran with 3 interior cabins, open deck space and comfort for cruising capacity of up to 18 guests, she is ideal for day or night charters. Outdoor enjoyment includes a kayak, snorkelling gear, dingy for beach excursions as well as a wide trampoline at the front bow for guests to sit on when Sunrise is underway. Guests may even sleep on the trampoline at night! With a very shallow keel, Sunrise may go into shallow water closer to the beaches. Sunrise also have a motorized dingy which can ferry people, food, and gear to the beach for lunch or a sunset dinner.​
