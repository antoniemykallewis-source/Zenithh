How to Choose the Right Type of Yacht| Zenith Yacht

If you’re planning a maritime adventure, you’ll quickly discover that the world of yachts is as expansive as the open sea itself. From elegant luxury yachts perfect for a romantic getaway to catamarans for a corporate gathering, selecting the right type of yacht can make all the difference. Keep reading to discover our selection tips to find the best yacht for your next voyage.

1. Types of vessels

First, let’s explore the types of vessels commonly available for charter in Singapore.

1. Monohull

A traditional single-hulled yacht, monohulls boast a narrow design and triangular-shaped bow, as well as a comfortable living space below deck. This type of yacht is popular among sailors who appreciate classic design and performance.

2. Sailing Monohull

A type of monohull propelled primarily by sails, sailing monohulls offer the thrill of sailing combined with the comfort of a well-designed vessel.

3. Sailing Catamaran

Sailing catamarans provide a stable and spacious platform, making them particularly popular with families and groups. Their generous deck space and comfortable living areas ensure ample room for relaxation and socialising.

4. Power Catamaran

Power catamarans are powered by engines instead of sails, offering a combination of speed, stability, and spaciousness. These are ideal for those who enjoy a comfortable and luxurious yachting experience without relying solely on wind power.

5. Superyacht

A spacious luxury yacht with multiple storeys and cabins, superyachts are equipped with high-end features like garages, gym rooms, spas, and even multiple recreation spaces.

2. Assess Your Needs

Next, assess your intended use of the yacht. Understanding your primary activity will help narrow down your options. For instance, if you plan to relax and sightsee with your family, consider the spaciousness and stability of a sailing catamaran. Fishing enthusiasts, on the other hand, would benefit from a fishing rental yacht equipped with features such as livewells, bait tanks, and fishing gear.

3. Size and Capacity

Next, consider the ideal size and capacity when choosing your vessel. This not only ensures the comfort of your journey but also the types of activities you can comfortably enjoy.

Make sure to consider the following:

  • Deck Space: For entertaining large groups, a yacht with ample deck space is essential. This space allows for social gatherings, sunbathing, and outdoor dining.
  • Onboard Facilities: If you plan to spend extended periods on the water, look for yachts with sufficient facilities, such as multiple cabins, bathrooms, and a well-equipped galley.
  • Passenger Capacity: Ensure the yacht can comfortably accommodate your usual number of guests. All yacht details, including the legal limit on maximum capacity, are typically provided upfront in the rental listings, so be sure to check this information before booking.

4. Consider Your Budget

Setting a realistic budget is crucial when selecting the right type of yacht. Superyachts, for instance, come with a premium price tag, starting at $4,000 onwards. Moreover, many superyachts do not offer half-day charters, providing overnight or longer-term rental options.

However, many affordable yacht rental options can meet your needs without sacrificing quality. With careful research, you can find a vessel that offers both comfort and value, ensuring a memorable experience on the water.
