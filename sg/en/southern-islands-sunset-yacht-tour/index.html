Southern Islands Sunset Yacht Tour

Enjoy a breath taking sunset tour while cruising on a luxurious Catamaran Yacht from Sentosa Cove Or the Republic of Singapore Yacht Club!

A. RSYC Route (Port and Petrochemical Historic Tour): Sail pass the Southern Islands of Singapore and be dazzied by the Port and Petrochemical History of Singapore. Learn about the history of PSA Port, Jurong Island, Pulau Bukom, and Pulau Semakau.

B. Sentosa Route (Southern Islands and City Skyline Tour): Learn about the interesting facts of Tekukor Island, Sisters Island Marine Park, St John’s Island, Kusu Island and Lazarus Island. Be impressed by the majestic view of the Marina Bay Financial Centre and learn about how it contributes to the Singapore economy.

Discover how these Islands and sites play a role in the development and overall success of Singapore
