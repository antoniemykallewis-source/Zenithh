6 Best Family Activities in Sentosa

While Sentosa is renowned as Singapore’s island playground, many may not realise the full extent of what it offers for families looking for a fun-filled, relaxing day out. If you’re planning a family getaway but unsure where to start, don’t worry—we’ve got you covered. From thrilling adventures to serene beach escapes, here are six must-try activities in Sentosa that promise something special for every family member.

1. Explore Universal Studios Singapore

No visit to Sentosa is complete without a visit to Universal Studios Singapore. The theme park has a range of family-friendly activities, including themed rides, shows, and attractions for kids. Dive into action-packed fun across different zones inspired by famous movies and TV shows, including Jurassic Park and Transformers. Younger children can experience the joys of gentler rides like the Sesame Street Spaghetti Space Chase and enjoy lively shows featuring their favourite characters. With so much to explore, it’s best to plan a full day here.

2. Make a Splash at Adventure Cove Waterpark

If you’re looking to beat the heat, head over to Adventure Cove Waterpark. The park features a mix of thrilling water slides, wave pools, and laid-back activities for children and adults alike. Take a plunge down the heart-stopping Pipeline Drop or delight in the twists and turns of the Riptide Rocket. Alternatively, relax and float down the gentle currents of the River Adventure or unwind in the serene atmosphere of the Bluwater Bay wave pool. Most importantly, don’t miss the chance to snorkel with tropical fish at the Rainbow Reef—a rare treat in Singapore.

3. Discover the Wonders of S.E.A. Aquarium

Spark a sense of wonder by visiting the S.E.A. Aquarium. Housing over 100,000 marine animals from over 800 species, the aquarium offers a captivating glimpse into the fascinating world beneath the waves. Witness the majesty of manta rays gliding through the Open Ocean tank, marvel at the colourful coral reefs teeming with life, and come face-to-face with majestic sharks. Plus, the interactive exhibits and educational displays provide an excellent opportunity for children to learn about marine conservation while having fun.

If fishing piques your interest, consider chartering a fishing boat for your family yacht trip. Experienced captains and crew will guide you to the best fishing spots in Singapore and share tips and techniques to help you and your loved ones reel in a great catch.

4. Relax at Palawan Beach

Palawan Beach provides a serene retreat for those seeking to relax by the sea. With its soft, golden sand and diverse range of amenities, Palawan is considered one of the best beaches in Sentosa. Build sandcastles with the little ones, take a refreshing dip in the crystal-clear waters, or participate in a friendly game of beach volleyball. This is a must-visit if you want to enjoy some downtime during your action-packed day.

5. Challenge Yourself at HydroDash

Looking for activities suitable for fun-seeking adults in Sentosa? Head over to Hydrodash, Singapore’s first floating water park. This adrenaline-pumping obstacle course features a series of inflatable slides, trampolines, balance beams, and climbing walls to test your agility, coordination, and sense of adventure. Children aged six and above can join in on the fun, making it an ideal activity for families with adventurous youngsters. Don’t forget to bring your swimwear and prepare for a day of high-energy fun!

6. Rent a Yacht for a Day

For those with a taste for luxury, renting a yacht for a day trip is the ultimate indulgence and an experience that transforms an ordinary family outing into an extraordinary adventure. Depart from ONE°15 Marina Sentosa Cove, one of the most prestigious marinas in Southeast Asia. From there, set sail on a delightful sunset cruise around Sentosa’s stunning coastline and explore the waters around the Southern Islands.

Onboard, you can enjoy various amenities that cater to your every need. From sun decks perfect for soaking up the sun to delicious dining options, every inch is designed for relaxation and enjoyment. And, as the evening unfolds, you can even catch the spectacular Wings of Time fireworks show that takes place on Siloso Beach, beautifully viewable from your yacht.

Whether you’re celebrating a special family occasion on a party boat or simply wish to relax with a unique waterfront adventure, a yacht trip is the perfect indulgent ending to your island getaway.
