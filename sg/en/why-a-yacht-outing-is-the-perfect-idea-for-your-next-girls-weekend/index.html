Why You Should Take a Yacht Trip with Your Girlfriends

Key Takeaways

  • There is no single answer to the question, “How much is a yacht?” The total price depends on several variables, including the vessel’s size, age, and amenities.
  • Expenses like insurance, marina berths, crew salaries, and maintenance can add up to tens of thousands annually.
  • Chartering a yacht, on the other hand, can be a practical alternative to ownership. It is cost-effective and convenient, though it often comes with limits on customisation, scheduling, and flexibility.
  • Yacht ownership offers control but demands a serious investment, while chartering delivers memorable experiences with none of the stress.

Why a Yacht Outing Is the Perfect Idea for Your Next Girls’ Trip in Singapore

Planning a trip with your girlfriends and looking for something more exciting than the usual brunch or staycation? Take your celebration to the waters with a private yacht experience! With stunning skyline views, hidden island escapes, and a luxurious setting all to yourselves, here are the top reasons why a yacht trip should be next on your bucket list.

Reason #1: Total Privacy for You and Your Besties

When you book a private yacht charter, it’s just you, your girls, and the open sea. No strangers, no crowded restaurants—just uninterrupted time together. Sunbathe on the deck, enjoy cocktails in the lounge, or belt out your favourite songs in the saloon. A yacht is like your own floating sanctuary, designed for laughter, deep conversations, and the kind of bonding that makes a trip with girlfriends unforgettable.

Reason #2: Instagram-Worthy Views and Vibes

Singapore’s skyline takes on a whole new magic when you see it from the water. Sail past Marina Bay Sands, drift towards Lazarus Island, or soak in golden hour while champagne glasses clink on deck. With the breeze in your hair and the city glittering in the background, every moment feels like a postcard. For those who love to capture and share memories, a yacht outing guarantees endless photo opportunities that will light up your social feeds. 

Reason #3: Built-In Fun Without the Planning Stress

Organising a trip with your girlfriends can feel overwhelming—coordinating schedules, activities, and food. But with Zenith Yacht Charters by your side, rest assured that everything will be taken care of for you. From curated itineraries and optional add-ons like yacht dining services and additional water toys, all you need to do is show up and enjoy the moment with your besties.

Reason #4: Surprisingly Affordable When You Split the Cost

Luxury doesn’t always mean breaking the bank. With group packages for 6 to 18 pax, a yacht outing with Zenith Yacht Charters often works out to around $100 per person—comparable to what you’d spend on a fancy night out. Bring your snacks and drinks, or opt for full-service catering on board. When you divide the cost, a yacht rental becomes a smart and surprisingly budget-friendly choice for a trip with girlfriends that feels far more memorable than yet another hotel staycation.

Reason #5: Perfect for Any Occasion—or No Occasion at All

One of the best things about a yacht outing is its flexibility. It’s ideal for birthdays, bachelorette parties, or milestone celebrations, but you don’t need a special reason to gather your favourite people. Sometimes, the best hangouts are the ones that let you escape the routine and create new stories together. Enjoy a tailor-made experience that feels as special as the friendships you’re celebrating.

Conclusion

If you’ve been searching for fun yet relaxing things to do with your friends, a private yacht trip might just be the answer. From total privacy and stunning views to built-in fun and affordability, it’s a combination of everything you could ever want. Whether it’s a milestone celebration or simply a long-overdue catch-up, Zenith Yacht Charters makes it easy to turn a trip with your girlfriends into something extraordinary.
