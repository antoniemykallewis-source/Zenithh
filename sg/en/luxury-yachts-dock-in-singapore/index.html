Where Do Luxury Yachts Dock in Singapore?

Setting sail on a private yacht charter in Singapore is the ultimate way to experience the city’s skyline and surrounding islands. But before you step aboard, you might wonder: Where exactly do these luxury vessels call home?

Understanding the marinas in Singapore helps you better plan your arrival, pick-up points, and overall yacht experience. Whether you are looking for a corporate team-building event or a romantic yacht wedding, knowing your departure point is the first step toward a seamless voyage.

Marina at Keppel Bay: The Jewel of Singapore Yachting

Marina at Keppel Bay is arguably the most prestigious docking location in the city. Located at the gateway to the southern islands, it offers world-class facilities and is the primary hub for many luxury yacht charters.

  • Why it’s popular: It provides easy, quick access to the open sea, making it perfect for day trips to Lazarus Island.
  • Amenities: With fine dining options and a stunning waterfront view, the pre-charter experience here is as luxurious as the cruise itself.

ONE°15 Marina Sentosa Cove

Located in the heart of the exclusive Sentosa Cove residential enclave, this marina is a hotspot for those looking for a premium yacht party experience.

  • The Vibe: It offers a more resort-style atmosphere.
  • Accessibility: It is perfect for guests who are already staying on Sentosa or want to incorporate a beach club visit into their itinerary.

Raffles Marina

Situated in the west, Raffles Marina serves as a gateway to the western islands. While it is further from the city center, it is a favorite for those seeking a more tranquil and secluded start to their fishing adventure or customized sailing tour.

How to Choose Your Departure Point

When you book a yacht with Zenith, our team handles the logistics. The best location often depends on:

  • Your Itinerary: Are you heading to the Southern Islands or staying near Marina Bay for an F1 cruise?
  • Convenience: Where are you and your guests based?
  • The Occasion: A birthday celebration might require a more accessible location for guests arriving by taxi or car.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to visit the marina before my charter?

No, you do not need to visit the marina in advance. Our team will provide clear instructions on the meeting point before your departure.

Which marina is best for a Marina Bay fireworks cruise?

Depending on your package, we often coordinate departures from central locations like Marina at Keppel Bay to ensure you have prime viewing for special events like New Year’s Eve.

Is there parking available at these marinas?

Yes, both Marina at Keppel Bay and ONE°15 Marina offer ample parking facilities for guests.

Can Zenith Yacht Charters help plan my route?

Absolutely! We specialize in curated itineraries that start and end at the most convenient marinas for your needs.
