Host Your Next Birthday Party on a Yacht

As we grow older, birthdays have gotten a bad reputation for reminding us about the checkpoints we expected to hit. It doesn’t have to be like that. Every trip around the sun should be worth remembering, surrounded by family and friends who want to celebrate you. When your birthday party is on a yacht, it’ll be hard to think about anything else. Let loose and give the ocean breeze a chance to blow out the candles.

Everything you yacht to know about birthday parties on sea

All in One

When you get a yacht, you are getting the venue, entertainment and decorations, all in one package. That means no more scrambling on Carousell or Reddit looking for cheap and good party decor options. It’s all settled. Just invite your people.

For Family and Furiends

Everyone dear to you should be on that boat. That includes your furkids. Our pet-friendly yacht charters allow you to bring your furry friends and give them the space to roam in safety. If you want to spoil your pets on your special day, we can cater a 4-course meal for your pets. It’s never too much.

For Every Budget

If you prefer a more intimate celebration, shorten your guest list and rightsize your choice of yacht. If anyone asks why they weren’t invited, putting it on the yacht capacity is always a safe option.

Tune in to Party

Music is an instant vibe creator. Whether it’s soft jazz or 90’s hits, indoor and outdoor speakers make it easy to make your party uniquely yours. When you’re in between songs, even the sound of the waves against the boat makes its own melody.

Birthday Party Yacht Feastivities: Picking the Menu

When you’re miles away from the shore, getting the right food is more important than ever. If for no other reason, it’s so that you won’t be finishing your birthday drinks on an empty stomach. At Zenith Yacht Charters, our dining options can fit any budget.

Barbeque

A barbecue dinner is quintessential birthday food. Except now, it is elevated from your Sweet 16 chalet to onboard a yacht. There is something about getting sizzling hot skewers straight off the grill that makes the food taste better.

Our standard BBQ platters include a Western BBQ menu, a vegetarian menu and a Chinese Chuan Chuan BBQ menu. Doesn’t tickle your fancy? We are happy to arrange for a menu that you can’t get enough of.

Buffet-Style Dining

Prefer a more balanced spread that will satisfy your whole family? We have halal mini buffet catering options that will make you feel like you never left home. Fit for multi-course meals or to satisfy itchy mouths with canapes, you will not be in want even when land is not in sight.

Private Chef

If you’re having a birthday party on a yacht, you might as well go all out. Our most premium dining option brings you a 1-Michelin-Star chef to cook up a storm. Expect no less than sashimi-grade Japanese oysters, a lamb rack, tomahawk, chilli crab on brioche toast and more. Or, eat fresh off a live grill with black angus striploin, miniburgers, prime ribeye and more. If that’s making your mouth water already, you might want to take a gulp before checking out the full menu here.
