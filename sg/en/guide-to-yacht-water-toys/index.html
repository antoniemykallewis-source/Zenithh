Ultimate Guide to Yacht Water Toys in Singapore

Planning a cruise out to Singapore’s Southern Islands? While lounging on the deck with a glass of champagne is an unforgettable experience, nothing turns a great day at sea into an extraordinary adventure quite like yacht water toys.

Whether you’re looking to zip across Lazarus Island’s turquoise bays on a jet ski or kick back on a giant floating mat with friends, choosing the right water sports setup is the key to an action-packed charter. In this guide, we break down the top high-speed thrillers, relaxing inflatables, and family-friendly gadgets available on a Zenith Yacht Charters cruise.

High-Adrenaline Thrillers: For the Ultimate Wave Hunters

If your ideal charter involves high speeds, splash zones, and an adrenaline rush, high-powered motorized water toys are a must-have addition to your itinerary.

1. Jet Skis

Nothing says ultimate excitement like a jet ski. Zipping across the open water gives you an unparalleled view of the coastline and surrounding islands.
Best For: Speed lovers and thrill-seekers.

Good to Know: In Singapore waters, operating a jet ski solo requires a Powered Pleasure Craft Driving Licence (PPCDL). Don’t have one? No problem! Most charters allow guests to ride pillion with a licensed crew member.

Charter Pairings: Check out motorized vessels like Zen Sea II, Seawind, MS Eternity I, Eaglewings V which feature add-on jet ski options.

2. E-Foils & Electric Surfboards

An electric hydrofoil (E-Foil) allows you to fly above the water’s surface at exhilarating speeds without needing wind or waves.

Best For: Surfers, snowboarders, and tech-loving adventurers.

Experience Level: Beginner to Intermediate (instructors can guide you on board).

3. Seabobs & Underwater Sea Scooters

Want to move through the water like a dolphin? A Seabob or Sublue Sea Scooter pulls you effortlessly through the water, allowing both surface cruising and underwater diving.

Best For: Snorkelers and swimming enthusiasts.

Highlight: Compact and easy for both adults and older kids to maneuver safely.

Chill & Unwind: Relaxing Inflatables & Lounge Toys

Not everyone wants to go 30 knots across the ocean. If your group prefers soaking up the sun, swimming, and socializing, these chill-out water toys will keep everyone entertained.

1. Large Water Mats & Floating Islands

A staple on almost every private charter, large floating water mats create an extension of the yacht’s deck right on the sea.

  • Why You’ll Love It: You can lie back, tan, sip drinks, and dip in the cool ocean water without constantly swimming.
  • Explore: Discover yachts equipped with complimentary water mats via our Yacht Fleet Collection.

2. Stand-Up Paddleboards (SUP) & Kayaks

If you want to explore hidden lagoons around Lazarus or St. John’s Island, taking out a paddleboard or kayak offers a peaceful, scenic workout.

Best For: Eco-touring and peaceful photo opportunities.

3. Inflatable Giant Water Slides

Turn your luxury yacht into a mini waterpark! Inflatable slides attach to the upper deck/flybridge, sending riders plunging straight into the ocean.

Best For: Birthday parties, corporate events, and family charters with kids.

How to Choose the Right Water Toys for Your Yacht Charter

When booking your Singapore Yacht Charter Package, consider these three quick tips to select the ideal toys:

  • Group Demographics: Families with young children often prefer floating mats, kayaks, and slides. Corporate groups or younger crowds love jet skis, e-foils, and sound systems on deck.
  • Charter Duration: For a standard 4-hour charter, 2–3 versatile water toys (e.g., a floating mat + paddleboard + jet ski) ensure everyone gets turn-time without feeling rushed.
  • Yacht Capacity & Storage: Larger motorized yachts can house heavier equipment like jet skis or Seabobs. Browse our Large Event Yachts to find vessels equipped for heavy water sport gear.

Safety First: Essential Rules for Water Sports at Sea

Your safety is always our top priority. Here are basic rules to keep in mind:

  • Life Jackets Are Mandatory: Guests engaging in water sports or swimming from the yacht are provided and requested to wear approved life jackets.
  • Follow Captain & Crew Briefings: Our experienced crew will guide you on safety zones, designated swimming areas, and toy operations.
  • Licensing & Age Limits: Motorized high-speed toys are subject to age restrictions and licensing laws. Check our FAQ Section or consult our charter team before adding motorized options.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are water toys included in my yacht charter price?

Many charters include basic complimentary water toys like floating mats, paddleboards, and kayaks. Premium motorized toys (such as Jet Skis, E-Foils, and Seabobs) or Giant Slides can be added as custom charter options. Check individual listings under our Yacht Fleet for details.

Can I drive a Jet Ski myself during the charter?

In Singapore, you must possess a valid Powered Pleasure Craft Driving Licence (PPCDL) to operate a jet ski independently. If you do not have a licence, you can still enjoy riding pillion with a certified crew member driving.

Are water toys safe for young children?

Yes! Non-motorized toys like large water mats, floating rings, and double-kayaks are family favorites. Zenith Yacht Charters provides child-sized life jackets for all young guests onboard.
