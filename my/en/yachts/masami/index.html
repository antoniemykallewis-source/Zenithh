Masami

Sailing Catamaran · 36 ft · Up to 15 guests

Your private yacht in Malaysia

Step aboard Masami.

MASAMI is a 36ft sailing catamaran in Langkawi, ideal for private outings with small groups of up to 15 guests. Departing from Royal Langkawi Yacht Club, the 4-hour charter includes local cuisine, soft drinks, beer and liquor, along with a paddleboard, seafloat and jacuzzi net. Snorkeling equipment and fishing gear are also available upon request. With packages starting from RM2,180 for 8 guests, Masami offers a relaxed way to explore Langkawi’s waters with family or friends.
