Sea Queen

Monohull · 60 ft · Up to 30 guests

Your private yacht in Malaysia

Step aboard Sea Queen.

SEA QUEEN is an elegant 60ft Sunseeker yacht in Penang, available for private charters of up to 30 guests. Departing from Straits Quay Marina, Sea Queen offers everything from an evening buffet cruise to a 4-hour journey along the Penang Bridge route. Standard inclusions include a buffet, drinking water and karaoke, with paddleboards, fishing rods and additional food and beverages available as add-ons. With packages starting from RM3,886, Sea Queen is well suited to celebrations, family outings, corporate gatherings and private cruises around Penang.
