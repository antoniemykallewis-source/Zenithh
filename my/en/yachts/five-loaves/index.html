Five Loaves

Power Catamaran · 48 ft · Up to 16 guests

Your private yacht in Malaysia

Step aboard Five Loaves.

FIVE LOAVES is a 48ft power catamaran in Penang, ideal for private parties, celebrations and group outings. Departing from Straits Quay Marina, the yacht accommodates up to 36 guests and includes onboard karaoke, with optional catering, cocktails, fishing, water sports, DJ sessions, live bands and event photography available. Private 3-hour charters start from RM4,088, making Five Loaves a flexible choice for entertaining on Penang’s waters.
