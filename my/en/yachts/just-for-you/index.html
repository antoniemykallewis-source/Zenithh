Just For You

Sailing Catamaran · 40 ft · Up to 20 guests

Your private yacht in Malaysia

Step aboard Just For You.

JUST FOR YOU is a 40ft sailing catamaran in Langkawi, offering private sunset cruises for up to 20 guests. Departing from Jeti Pelancongan Pekan Rabu, this relaxed 4-hour charter includes local cuisine, drinks, a paddleboard and jacuzzi net, with optional jet ski and jet car activities available. Packages start from RM3,500 for up to 15 guests, making JUST FOR YOU a comfortable choice for families, groups and private celebrations on Langkawi’s waters.
