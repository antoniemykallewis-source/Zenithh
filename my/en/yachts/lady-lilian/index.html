Lady Lilian

Power Catamaran · 60 ft · Up to 36 guests

Your private yacht in Malaysia

Step aboard Lady Lilian.

LADY LILIAN is a spacious 60ft power catamaran in Penang, designed for private group charters of up to 36 guests. Departing from Straits Quay Marina, the yacht is well suited to corporate events, birthday celebrations, family gatherings and other private occasions. Catering, beverages, customised décor and live-band setups can be arranged, while optional activities include kayaking, fishing, paddleboarding and an inflatable platform. Private 4-hour charters start from RM7,290 on weekdays.
