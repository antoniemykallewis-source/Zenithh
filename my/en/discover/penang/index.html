Discover the Highlights of Penang

Penang blends colonial heritage with a lively coastline, offering a mix of historic charm and natural island escapes. Home to George Town’s UNESCO-listed streets and the twin Penang Bridges, this vibrant island destination is best appreciated from the water, where the city skyline meets untouched coastal wilderness just a short cruise away. As a experts in yacht charters, Zenith Yacht Charters offers unforgettable luxury yacht charter experiences in Penang, Malaysia.

Penang
Monkey Beach

Tucked within Penang National Park, Monkey Beach is a secluded stretch of coastline reachable only by boat. True to its name, the beach is home to playful long-tailed macaques, alongside sightings of green turtles and white-bellied sea eagles in the surrounding waters.

Penang
Penang Bridge Route

Cruising south along the east coast offers sweeping views of George Town’s skyline and the two iconic Penang Bridges. It’s one of the most scenic routes on the island, especially popular for a Penang sunset cruise or evening on the water.

Penang
Pulau Rimau

Cruising past Pulau Rimau offers views of its rugged, forested coastline and historic lighthouse, built in 1885 to guide vessels approaching Penang. This uninhabited islet adds a distinctive maritime landmark to a yacht journey along Penang’s southern coast.

Penang
Batu Ferringhi & Tanjung Bungah

Known for their five-star resorts and clear coastal waters, Batu Ferringhi and Tanjung Bungah are popular beachfront stretches along Penang’s north coast, offering a scenic backdrop for cruising past the island’s shoreline.

Penang
Song Song & Bidan Islands

For those venturing further into Kedah, Pulau Song Song and Pulau Bidan offer a quieter, more remote island escape, with clear waters well suited to fishing and a more secluded overnight stop.
