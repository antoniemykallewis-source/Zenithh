Langkawi Yacht Charter

Discover the Highlights of Langkawi

Langkawi is Malaysia’s premier island destination, celebrated for its pristine beaches, lush rainforests, dramatic limestone formations, and crystal-clear waters. As a leading name for chartering yachts in Malaysia, Zenith Yacht Charters offers unforgettable Langkawi yacht charter experiences in Malaysia, giving you access to this UNESCO Global Geopark’s most breathtaking natural wonders — all from the comfort of a private yacht.

Langkawi
Dayang Bunting Island

Home to the famous Lake of the Pregnant Maiden, Dayang Bunting Island is surrounded by lush rainforest and towering limestone hills. Visitors can enjoy swimming in the freshwater lake, kayaking, or simply taking in the island’s tranquil natural beauty.

Langkawi
Pulau Singa Besar

Known for its rich wildlife and unspoiled scenery, Pulau Singa Besar offers opportunities to spot majestic eagles, monkeys, and monitor lizards. The surrounding waters are perfect for relaxing cruises and nature photography.

Langkawi
Beras Basah Island

A favourite scenic stop, Beras Basah Island features soft white-sand beaches, clear turquoise waters, and excellent swimming conditions. It’s an ideal destination for sunbathing, beach walks, and enjoying a tropical island escape.

Langkawi
Kilim Karst Geoforest Park

Explore Langkawi’s spectacular mangrove forests, towering limestone cliffs, hidden caves, and diverse wildlife at Kilim Karst Geoforest Park. Cruising through this UNESCO-listed landscape offers one of the island’s most unforgettable experiences.

Langkawi
Pulau Intan Kecil

A secluded island surrounded by calm, crystal-clear waters, Pulau Intan Kecil is perfect for swimming, snorkeling, and enjoying the peaceful beauty of Langkawi away from the crowds.
