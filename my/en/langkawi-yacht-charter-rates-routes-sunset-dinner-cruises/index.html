Langkawi Yacht Charter: Rates, Routes & Sunset Cruises

Few places in Southeast Asia reward a day on the water quite like Langkawi. This UNESCO Global Geopark of 99 islands off Malaysia’s west coast is made for exploring by boat — and a private yacht is by far the most comfortable way to do it. Whether you’re weighing up half-day pricing, planning an island-hopping route, or dreaming of a sunset dinner cruise, this guide breaks down everything you need to know before you book with Zenith Yacht Charters Malaysia. From rates to itineraries to what to expect on board, we’ll walk you through it so you can pick the right yacht from our fleet with confidence.

Why Charter a Yacht in Langkawi?

Langkawi’s appeal comes from its geography. The archipelago sits close to the Thai border, its waters dotted with limestone karsts, mangrove rivers, and quiet beaches that are only reachable by boat. Chartering a private yacht means you’re not tied to a fixed group tour schedule — you choose the islands, the pace, and how long you linger at each stop. For a deeper look at what makes the destination special, our Discover Langkawi page covers the geography, marine life, and best times of year to sail. In short: if you want swimming, snorkeling, and postcard views without the crowds of a shared speedboat tour, a private charter is the way to go.

Langkawi Yacht Charter Rates: What You Can Expect to Pay

Half-Day, Full-Day & Overnight Pricing

Langkawi yacht charter rates vary depending on the size of the vessel, the length of the trip, and how many guests you’re bringing along. As a general guide:

  • Half-day charters (around 4 hours) suit smaller groups wanting a quick taste of the water — swimming, light refreshments and extraordinary views.
  • Full-day charters (6–8 hours) allow time for fishing or snorkeling breaks, more time for water activities, an onboard lunch and a great sunset view.
  • Overnight charters are reserved for larger yachts and give you the rare experience of waking up anchored off a private bay.

Rates typically start from around RM2,180 for a mid-sized motor yacht and scale up for larger group and sunset charters, so it’s worth matching the vessel to your group size and budget rather than assuming bigger is always better.

What Affects Your Charter Rate

A few factors move the price up or down: the yacht’s size and class, the marina it departs from, the duration of your booking, add-ons like food and beverage packages, water toys and whether you’re chartering during the morning or evening, on a weekday versus a weekend. On the more intimate end, the RUBYLITE — a 31ft motor yacht based at the Royal Langkawi Yacht Club — is a comfortable option for families and small groups, with packages starting from RM2,180. For larger celebrations or events, the EAGLE MAGIC 3, a 57ft Power Catamaran based at Jeti Pelancongan Pekan Rabu, accommodates up to 36 guests with a jacuzzi net, paddleboards, local cuisine, and cocktails included as standard, with packages from RM10,000. Between these two, most group sizes and budgets are covered.

Best Langkawi Yacht Charter Routes

The Eagle Square Route

This is the classic Langkawi day out: departing from the marina, cruising past the iconic Eagle statue, and stopping to swim or snorkel in calm, sheltered bays. It’s an easy-going route that works well for families and first-time charter guests, and it can be shortened or extended depending on how much time you have on board.

Dayang Bunting & Pulau Payar Route

Dayang Bunting, known for its freshwater lake, and Pulau Payar, known for its coral reefs, are two of the most requested stops on a full-day charter. This route is better suited to a full-day or longer booking, since travel time between the outer islands is greater than the inner-island hopping route.

Extend Your Trip with a Tanjung Rhu Mangrove Tour

The Kilim Karst Geoforest and mangrove waterways are one of Langkawi’s most scenic areas, but the terrain there is only navigable by smaller speedboats rather than a private yacht. Guests wanting both experiences often pair their yacht charter with a separate Tanjung Rhu mangrove tour, which covers highlights like the mangrove swamp, Bat Cave, Crocodile Cave, and eagle feeding by speedboat. Ask our team when booking and we can help coordinate both legs of your trip.

Sunset Dinner Cruises in Langkawi

What's Included on Board

A sunset dinner cruise is one of the most popular ways to charter in Langkawi, and for good reason — watching the sky turn orange over the Andaman Sea from your own deck is hard to beat. Most sunset charters run for a few hours in the late afternoon and evening, and typically include a set or buffet-style dinner, drinking water, and time anchored somewhere scenic to enjoy the view before heading back to the marina.

Choosing the Right Yacht for Your Sunset Cruise

Smaller groups often prefer the intimacy of a motor yacht like Rubylite, while larger celebrations — engagements, birthdays, or corporate send-offs — tend to book a bigger vessel with more deck space and amenities. Frequently Asked Questions

1. How much does a Langkawi yacht charter cost?

Prices generally start at around RM2,180 for a smaller motor yacht on a half-day or full-day charter, rising to RM10,000 for a larger-group sunset charter with more onboard amenities.

2. How many guests can a Langkawi yacht charter accommodate?

This depends on the vessel — smaller motor yachts typically hold up to around 12 guests, while a larger power catamaran like Eagle Magic 3 can accommodate up to 36 guests, excluding the captain and crew.

3. What's included in a sunset dinner cruise?

Most sunset dinner cruises include a set or buffet dinner, select beverage options and water activities while overlooking the sunset.

4. What is the best route for first-time charter guests in Langkawi?

The scenic route around Langkawi’s iconic Eagle Square is a gentle introduction, with calm waters and a few short stops for swimming and snorkeling.

5. Which marina do Langkawi charters depart from?

Zenith’s Langkawi yachts, including Rubylite, are berthed at the Royal Langkawi Yacht Club, while others like Eagle Magic 3 meet at Jeti Pelancongan Pekan Rabu.

6. How far in advance should I book a Langkawi yacht charter?

It’s best to book at least a few weeks ahead, especially for weekends, public holidays, or larger yachts, since availability for popular dates fills up quickly.
