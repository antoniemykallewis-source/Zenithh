Explore Malaysia’s Island & Coastal Destinations

Discover tropical islands, secluded beaches and scenic coastlines with an itinerary designed around how you want to spend your time on the water.

Penang

Sailing from Straits Green Park near Tanjung Tokong or Pantai Seagate Bayan Lepas, our Penang charters offer scenic cruises along the Penang Strait, sunset trips past the Penang Bridge, day excursions to Monkey Beach or Song Song Island for snorkeling and fishing.

Explore Penang

Langkawi

Departing from Royal Langkawi Yacht Club or Jeti Pelancongan Pekan Rabu, explore the 99-island archipelago, UNESCO Global Geopark, Kilim Karst Geoforest Park, and Dayang Bunting on private day or sunset cruises.

Explore Langkawi

Johor

Sailing from Puteri Harbour in Iskandar Puteri, enjoy private cruises along the Johor Strait for the best sunset view.

Explore Johor

Tioman

Cruise pristine tropical waters near Tekek Marina toward Pulau Tulai (Coral Island) for snorkeling, coral reef exploration, and secluded white-sand beach getaways.

Explore Tioman

Rompin

Operating out of Kuala Rompin Jetty, our Rompin charters provide an ideal base for seasonal sailfish game fishing and open-water coastal cruising.

Explore Rompin

Sipadan

Departing near Semporna and Mabul Island, explore East Malaysia’s tropical archipelagos and world-famous diving locations surrounding the Sipadan region.

Explore Sipadan

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in a standard yacht charter?

Inclusions vary depending on the vessel and package selected. Your quotation will clearly outline the charter duration, location, included amenities and any activities provided. Optional services such as catering, entertainment and additional water activities can also be arranged for selected packages.

Where do your yacht charters depart from in Malaysia?

Departure point will vary depending on your desired destination. Custom departure arrangements may also be available depending on the selected vessel, destination, duration and operational requirements. Contact us on where you would like to go and our team will advise on suitable options.

Can we bring our own food and beverages (BYO)?

Yes. However, selected yachts may charge a corkage or cleaning fee. Let us know your preferences when requesting a quotation and our team will explain the available options.

What happens in the event of bad weather?

Guest safety is and will always be our priority. Weather and sea conditions are monitored before departure, and the captain will determine whether conditions are suitable for the planned itinerary.

If conditions prevent the trip from proceeding as scheduled, our team will discuss the available options with you, which may include adjusting the route or rescheduling in accordance with your booking terms.

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend booking early, particularly for weekends, public holidays, school holidays and popular celebration dates. Availability varies by vessel and season. If your dates are flexible, our team can suggest suitable options and alternative departure times.

How many guests can you accommodate?

Our fleet includes options for couples and small private groups through to larger events. Capacity varies by vessel and may differ between day trips and overnight stays. Share your group size with us and we will recommend suitable options.

How much does it cost to charter or rent a private yacht in Malaysia?

Pricing depends on the vessel, duration, destination, number of guests and any additional services requested. Selected all-inclusive day packages start from RM2,640.

Contact our team with your preferred date, group size and itinerary for an accurate quotation.

Can we customize our itinerary?

Yes. Your itinerary can be tailored around your preferred destinations, duration and activities, subject to weather, sea conditions and operational requirements.

If you are unsure where to go, our team can recommend routes based on the experience you are looking for.
